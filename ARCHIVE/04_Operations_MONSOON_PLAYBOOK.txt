2. MONSOON PRICING STRATEGY
================================================================================

OBJECTIVE: Protect margins while keeping delivery attractive.

RAIN SURCHARGE (Optional / Tiered)
  - Light rain (drizzle): No surcharge
  - Moderate rain: BDT 20 surcharge per order (auto-applied if weather alert active)
  - Heavy rain / waterlogging: BDT 50 surcharge + ETA extension
  - Communicate clearly: "A small rain surcharge helps us keep your order safe and dry. 🙏"

MINIMUM ORDER ADJUSTMENT
  - Normal: BDT 300 minimum
  - Monsoon: Raise to BDT 400 (offset delivery risk + bag costs)
  - Exempt COD orders < BDT 400 if customer prepays bKash (encourages digital)

BUNDLED OFFERS (Revenue Protection)
  - "Rainy Day Combo": Rice 5kg + lentils + onions + garlic = BDT 850 (save BDT 120)
  - "Indoor Essentials Bundle": Floor cleaner + mosquito coil + tissue + soap = BDT 450
  - "Hot & Cozy Pack": Instant noodles + soup + tea + biscuits = BDT 380
  - "No-Cook Dinner Box": Bread + butter + jam + eggs + condensed milk = BDT 320

PROMOTIONS TO DRIVE VOLUME
  - "Monsoon Loyalty": 5th order free delivery (track via WhatsApp sticker or code)
  - "Pre-order Bonus": Order before 4PM for next-day 7AM guaranteed slot
  - "Group Order Discount": 3+ orders to same address = 20% off delivery fee

PRICE FREEZE COMMITMENT
  - Do NOT raise item prices during monsoon (only delivery fee adjustment allowed)
  - If supplier raises prices, absorb first BDT 50 increase per item; pass rest transparently

3. DELIVERY BUFFER ADJUSTMENTS
================================================================================

NORMAL BUFFER → MONSOON BUFFER
  - Normal ETA: 30–40 mins → Monsoon ETA: 45–60 mins
  - Normal cutoffs: 10AM, 2PM, 5PM → Monsoon cutoffs: 9AM, 1PM, 4:30PM (cut 30 mins earlier)
  - Normal batch: every 3 hours → Monsoon batch: every 2 hours (smaller, more frequent)
  - Normal orders per rider: 15–20 → Monsoon orders per rider: 10–12 (safety first)

DELIVERY WINDOW SLOTS (Monsoon)
  Slot 1: 07:00 – 09:00 (early bird, dry window)
  Slot 2: 09:30 – 11:30 (pre-rain window)
  Slot 3: 12:00 – 14:00 (light rain expected, higher surcharge)
  Slot 4: 15:00 – 17:00 (afternoon break, if rain stops)
  Slot 5: 17:30 – 19:30 (evening, if dry)

CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION
  - WhatsApp broadcast at 06:30 daily: "Today's weather: [rain/dry]. Delivery slots: [list]. Order now!"
  - If rain starts mid-day: Broadcast "Heavy rain alert. Next delivery at [next slot time]."
  - Real-time updates: "Rider [Name] is delayed by 20 mins due to waterlogging at [location]."

RIDER SAFETY RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
  - No riding in water deeper than ankle height (engine + electrical risk)
  - Maximum speed 25 km/h in rain (vs. 35 km/h normal)
  - Avoid underpasses prone to flooding (map known flood points)
  - If lightning within 5 km: Stop, wait 30 mins under shelter, do not resume
  - Phone in waterproof pouch at all times; no calls while riding

4. WEATHER CANCELLATION PROTOCOL
================================================================================

FOUNDER DECISION ONLY
  Only Founder (Fahad Ibrahim) can declare "Weather Hold."
  Criteria:
    - Bangladesh Meteorological Department warning: heavy rain / thunderstorm
    - Road conditions: waterlogging > 12 inches on main routes
    - Rider safety: any rider reports unsafe conditions

CANCELLATION PROCESS
  1. Founder sends WhatsApp broadcast to ALL pending customers: "Weather Hold. Your order [ID] is rescheduled to [next slot]. Full refund if you prefer. Reply YES to confirm new time or NO for refund."
  2. Ops Lead updates Order Tracker: "Weather Hold – Rescheduled" or "Weather Hold – Refunded"
  3. Partners notified: "Weather Hold. Do not prepare orders for [time window]."
  4. If cancellation > 2 hours before delivery: Full refund (no penalty)
  5. If cancellation < 2 hours: Full refund + BDT 50 credit coupon for next order

CUSTOMER REFUND PROCESS
  - COD customers: Refund via bKash (ask for number) or cash at next delivery
  - bKash customers: Auto-refund to same TrxID within 2 hours
  - Document all refunds in Financial Log under "Weather Hold Refunds"

BUSINESS CONTINUITY DURING WEATHER HOLD
  - Hub remains open for inquiries
  - WhatsApp auto-reply updated: "Weather Hold in effect. We'll resume once roads are safe."
  - Ops Lead monitors weather every 30 mins via BMD app / local news
  - Resume decision: Founder announces "All Clear" + first delivery slot

6. REVENUE PROTECTION STRATEGIES
================================================================================

A. PIVOT TO IN-DEMAND MONSOON ITEMS
  Stock heavily during June–September:
    - Cooking: Rice, lentils, onions, garlic, ginger, turmeric, mustard oil
    - Beverages: Tea leaves, coffee, milk packets, bottled water
    - Snacks: Biscuits, chips, noodles, chanachur (non-perishable)
    - Household: Detergent, dish soap, floor cleaner, mosquito coils, electric mats
    - Baby: Diapers, baby food, formula (parents avoid going out)

B. BUNDLED OFFERS (See Section 2)
  - Push bundles via WhatsApp broadcast at 11:00 and 16:00
  - Offer "Monsoon Survival Kit" as fixed-price package
  - Partner with P001 (dry goods) and P005 (bakery) for exclusive bundles

C. PRE-ORDER MODEL
  - "Order tonight, receive tomorrow 7AM" – reduces same-day weather risk
  - Offer 5% discount for pre-orders placed after 8PM
  - Use pre-orders to smooth demand and reduce rider idle time

D. CORPORATE / BULK ORDERS
  - Target JGC apartment buildings for bulk orders (rice, oil, water)
  - Offer weekly subscription: "Weekly Essentials" delivered every Monday 7AM
  - Fixed revenue stream, less weather-dependent

E. DOOR-STEP STATION STRATEGY
  - If rain is persistent, set up temporary pickup point under JGC Central shelter
  - Customers collect from shelter (no delivery fee if they walk 200m)
  - Saves rider time + fuel; keeps revenue flowing

F. COST CONTAINMENT
  - Reduce rider shifts during heavy rain (split shifts: 6–10AM + 3–7PM instead of 6–8PM continuous)
  - Use bKash more aggressively (COD risky in rain, cash handling slower)
  - Minimize perishable purchases during peak rain weeks (reduce spoilage)
  - Negotiate extended payment terms with partners during monsoon

8. MONSOON SAFETY REMINDERS (Post in Hub)
================================================================================

RIDER SAFETY
  • Helmet ON before engine ON
  • Phone in waterproof pouch – no exceptions
  • If lightning: stop under shelter, wait 30 mins
  • No flooded roads – even if shortcut looks passable
  • Slow down – wet roads double stopping distance
  • Wear bright/reflective jacket in heavy rain

CUSTOMER SAFETY
  • If customer opens door in heavy rain: step back, hand items under shelter/roof
  • Never enter customer home (policy: doorstep only)
  • Sanitize hands before/after every delivery
  • If customer is elderly/sick: offer to leave items at designated spot

FOOD SAFETY
  • Perishables in cool bag + ice pack at all times
  • If cool bag fails: discard perishables, do not deliver
  • Raw meat/fish: double-bag, separate from ready-to-eat items
  • Discard any item that got wet (packaging compromised)
