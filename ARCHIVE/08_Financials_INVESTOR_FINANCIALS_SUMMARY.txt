6. COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Advantage               Why It's Defensible
──────────────────────  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Distance                800m to Krishi Market vs Chaldal's 5km+ warehouses.
                        15-min pickup vs 2-hr dispatch. Cannot be replicated
                        without physical presence.
Cost Structure          Zero warehouse, zero inventory, zero app development.
                        Chaldal burns $30M+ on 27 warehouses. We burn 0.
Community Trust         WhatsApp-native, resident founder, building committee
                        relationships. Platform apps cannot buy this.
Speed                   2-hour delivery guarantee. Chaldal: 3-5 hours to
                        Mohammadpur. Speed is the primary purchase driver.
Pricing                 Krishi Market prices + 11% commission. Chaldal marks
                        up 25-40% to cover warehouse costs. We don't.

8. EXIT SCENARIOS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Scenario 1: Strategic Acquisition (Most Likely)
• Acquirer: Chaldal, Foodpanda, Pathao, or international grocery chain
• Trigger: Proven unit economics + 1,000+ customers + adjacent market expansion
• Timeline: 24-36 months
• Expected Return: 5-10x on seed investment
• Valuation at exit: BDT 5-10 Crore

Scenario 2: Organic Growth (Base Case)
• Self-funded expansion to 5,000+ customers
• Annual revenue BDT 8-10 Crore, profit BDT 1.5-2 Crore
• Series A at BDT 20-30 Crore valuation
• Seed investors can sell partial stake or hold

Scenario 3: Franchise/Platform (Aggressive)
• Replicate JGC model across 20+ Dhaka gated complexes
• SaaS-like revenue from franchise fees + commission
• Valuation at exit: BDT 50+ Crore
• Timeline: 36-48 months

9. INVESTMENT TERMS

Maturity: 24 months

Information Rights: Quarterly financial reports, annual board update
Pro-rata Rights: Yes
Most Favored Nation: Yes

Why SAFE over equity: Keeps cap table clean, defers valuation negotiation,
and is standard for pre-seed in Bangladesh. Investors get standard protection
through the cap and discount.
