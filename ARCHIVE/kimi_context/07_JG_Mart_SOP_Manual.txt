SECTION 2: ORDER PROCESSING SOP
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1: RECEIVE ORDER
─────────────────────
• Customer sends WhatsApp message with item list
• Example: "Order: 1kg Chinigura rice, 500g dal, 2L oil, 1kg potato, 6 eggs"
• Manager copies exact text into Google Sheet "Orders" tab within 2 minutes

STEP 2: CONFIRM RECEIPT (Auto-Reply)
─────────────────────────────────────
Send this exact message:

"Thank you for your order! 
Order #JG[00123] received.
Estimated total: [450-520] BDT.
We'll confirm exact total by [9:15AM / 3:45PM].
Delivery: Today [12PM / 8PM] slot.
Reply CANCEL to cancel."

STEP 3: CALCULATE & CONFIRM TOTAL
──────────────────────────────────
• Check partner prices from "Product Table"
• Add delivery fee (if applicable)
• Apply subscription discount (if subscriber)
• Send confirmation message:

"Order #JG[00123] confirmed: [487] BDT
Items: [list with prices]
Delivery: [30 BDT / FREE]
Total: [487] BDT
Pay now: [bKash number] or COD
Reply PAID after payment"

STEP 4: SPLIT BY PARTNER
────────────────────────
• Use "Order Splitting" column in Sheet
• Assign each item to correct partner
• Send individual WhatsApp to each partner:

"Order from JG Mart:
Chinigura Rice 1kg x 95 = 95
Moshur Dal 500g x 65 = 65
Total: 160 BDT
Pickup: 10:00-11:00 AM
Please confirm: YES / NO"

STEP 5: RIDER PICKUP
────────────────────
• Rider receives printed/sheet list of all orders
• Goes to each partner in sequence
• Checks quality before accepting
• Takes photo of any questionable items
• Signs off on partner's copy (or WhatsApp confirmation)

STEP 6: SORTING AT HUB
──────────────────────
• Lay out all items on clean table
• Group by customer order
• Check against order list
• Pack in JG Mart branded bags
• Attach order number sticker to each bag

STEP 7: DELIVERY
────────────────
• Follow optimized route (Cluster 1 → 2 → 3 → 4)
• Call customer 5 min before arrival
• Hand over bag, collect payment
• For COD: Count cash on spot, give receipt
• For bKash: Verify transaction ID
• Mark "Delivered" in Sheet immediately

STEP 8: END-OF-DAY RECONCILIATION
──────────────────────────────────
• Count total cash collected
• Match against Sheet "Payment" column
• Deposit cash to bank or keep as float
• Update "Financial Tracker" tab
• Flag any discrepancies

SECTION 4: PARTNER MANAGEMENT SOP
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DAILY PARTNER CHECK-IN:
• 06:30 AM: Send availability request
• 09:30 AM: Confirm morning orders
• 04:00 PM: Confirm evening orders
• 09:00 PM: Send next-day forecast

WEEKLY PARTNER REVIEW (Every Sunday):
• Calculate total orders per partner
• Calculate commission earned
• Review reliability score (on-time %)
• Discuss any issues or price changes
• Pay weekly settlement (cash or bKash)

PARTNER RELIABILITY SCORING:
• 100 points = Perfect week (all orders on time, no issues)
• -10 points per late confirmation
• -20 points per wrong item
• -50 points per missed order
• Partners below 70 points get warning
• Partners below 50 points get replaced

BACKUP PARTNER PROTOCOL:
• Maintain 2 backup partners per category
• If primary fails, immediately switch to backup
• Inform customer only if item unavailable
• Never blame partner to customer

SECTION 6: QUALITY CONTROL CHECKLIST
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

AT PICKUP (Rider checks each item):
□ Vegetables: Fresh, no wilting, no spots
□ Fish: Eyes clear, gills red, no bad smell
□ Meat: Proper color, no discoloration
□ Rice/Dal: Correct brand, sealed package
□ Oil: Sealed, within expiry date
□ Eggs: No cracks, proper count
□ Fruits: Ripe but not overripe

AT SORTING (Manager verifies):
□ All items present vs. order list
□ Correct quantities
□ Items grouped by customer
□ Bags properly sealed
□ Order number attached

AT DELIVERY (Rider confirms):
□ Correct customer and building
□ Correct order number
□ Bag sealed and clean
□ Payment collected correctly
□ Customer signature or confirmation

SECTION 7: EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RIDER ACCIDENT/INJURY:
1. Call rider immediately, assess situation
2. If minor: Send backup rider to complete route
3. If serious: Call emergency services, inform family
4. Manager takes over remaining deliveries
5. Document incident, review insurance

PARTNER FAILS TO DELIVER:
1. Immediately contact backup partner
2. If backup unavailable, source from market directly
3. Inform customer of 30-min delay
4. Offer 50 BDT credit for inconvenience
5. Document failure, adjust partner score

CUSTOMER REFUSES PAYMENT:
1. Stay calm, do not argue
2. Accept item back if possible
3. Note customer ID, flag for review
4. Do NOT deliver to this customer again without prepayment
5. Report to manager immediately

WEATHER EMERGENCY (Heavy Rain/Flood):
1. Assess safety — rider safety first
2. If unsafe: Cancel deliveries, inform all customers
3. Offer full refund + 100 BDT credit
4. Reschedule for next available slot
5. Update WhatsApp status with weather notice

FOOD SAFETY INCIDENT:
1. Immediately stop all deliveries
2. Recall any affected orders
3. Full refund + medical expense coverage offer
4. Document everything
5. Review partner and sourcing protocol
