1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1.1 Problem Statement
---------------------
Japan Garden City (JGC) houses ~1,700 affluent families in 26 buildings (15-16
storeys each). The complex has ZERO internal grocery stores. Residents must:

• Exit the gated complex through Ring Road
• Navigate traffic jams caused by bus terminals
• Walk/bike 800m to Krishi Market in dust, noise, and heat
• Spend 45+ minutes for daily essentials
• Carry heavy items (rice, oil) back through the same conditions

The pain is not just time — it's the cumulative frustration of leaving a premium
residential complex to buy basic groceries like a refugee. JGC residents pay
75Lakh-2.6Cr BDT for flats, have special 33/11kv substations (no load-shedding),
and expect premium service. They are time-starved, stressed, and willing to pay
for convenience.

1.2 Product Vision
------------------
"Be the fastest, most trusted grocery delivery for JG City residents by leveraging
local shops and community trust. Deliver in 2 hours at Krishi Market prices."

1.3 Success Metrics - 90 Days
-----------------------------
Metric                          Target

Orders per Month                750
On-time Delivery Rate           >90%
Repeat Rate                     >35%
Net Profit                      >42,000 BDT/mo

4. OPERATIONAL WORKFLOW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4.1 Order Flow
--------------
06:30  Manager sends availability check to all partners via WhatsApp
07:00  Partners reply with unavailable items and today's prices
07:30  Manager updates "Available Today" catalog
08:00  Customer ordering window OPENS
09:00  MORNING CUTOFF — stop taking orders for 12PM slot
09:15  Manager splits orders by partner, sends WhatsApp to each partner
09:30  Partners confirm or flag issues (15-min deadline)
10:00  Rider departs for Krishi Market pickup
11:15  Rider returns to sorting hub (home)
11:30  Sort orders by building, check quality, pack in bags
11:45  Depart for deliveries — Cluster 1 (Building 1-6)
12:00  12PM DELIVERY SLOT BEGINS
12:45  Morning deliveries complete, collect COD, update Sheet

03:30  EVENING CUTOFF
03:45  Split evening orders, send to partners
05:30  Rider departs for evening pickup
08:00  8PM DELIVERY SLOT BEGINS
08:45  Evening deliveries complete

4.2 Data Model
--------------
Order Table:    OrderID, Customer, Block, Items[], Total, PartnerSplit[],
                Status, Rider, PaymentStatus, CustomerType, IssueFlag

Partner Table:  PartnerID, Name, Category, Commission%, Phone, DueAmount,
                ReliabilityScore, LastOrderDate, CreditLimit

Product Table:  SKU, Name, Price, Partner, Category, StockStatus

DailyInventory: PartnerID, SKU, AvailableQty, PriceToday, LastUpdated

5. PARTNER REQUIREMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Located within 2km of Japan Garden City (preferably Krishi Market)
2. WhatsApp Business account active and responsive
3. Able to confirm orders within 15 minutes during business hours
4. Willing to provide products at agreed wholesale + commission pricing
5. Available for pickup between 10:00-11:00 AM and 5:30-6:30 PM daily
6. Able to maintain consistent quality and stock levels
7. No history of fraud or customer complaints in last 12 months
8. Willing to sign 3-month minimum commitment
9. Must agree to photo-proof of fresh items (fish/veg)
10. Must allow JG Mart branded bags
