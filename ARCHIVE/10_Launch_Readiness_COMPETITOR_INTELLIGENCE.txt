EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

JG Mart competes in three arenas:
1. Local bazaar proximity (convenience vs cost)
2. City-wide delivery platforms (Chaldal, Foodpanda, Pathao)
3. Future grocery entrants (Shohoz, local copycats)

Our structural advantage: 800m sourcing distance + WhatsApp-native + community trust.
No competitor can replicate all three simultaneously.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. CHALDAL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Overview
--------
Bangladesh's largest online grocery platform. Raised $30M+. 27 warehouses nationwide.
Backed by Nitol-Niloy Group. Market leader in Dhaka.

Strengths
---------
• Massive SKU selection (10,000+)
• 30-min delivery promise in prime zones
• Strong brand recognition
• Professional app + web platform
• Integrated payments, subscriptions, loyalty

Weaknesses vs JG Mart
---------------------
• NO warehouse in Mohammadpur. Nearest: likely Hazaribag or Dhanmondi (5+ km).
• Delivery to JGC takes 3-5 hours minimum.
• High minimum order values (BDT 300-500)
• Delivery fees BDT 40-60 for non-prime zones
• Cash-flow crisis reported: workforce cut from 3,300 to 2,200 in 2025
• Impersonal app-based UX; no community relationship

JG Mart Counter-Strategy
------------------------
• Lead with speed: "2 hours vs 3-5 hours"
• Lead with proximity: "800m away vs 5km away"
• Lead with relationship: "Your neighbor delivers vs anonymous app"
• Lock in customers with WhatsApp subscriptions before Chaldal enters
• Build switching costs: loyalty program, referral credits, weekly habits

Threat Level: MEDIUM (6-12 month head start before they can serve JGC efficiently)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. FOODPANDA
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Overview
--------
Delivery Hero subsidiary. Restaurant delivery leader expanding into grocery.
Present in Dhaka with restaurant network. Grocery is secondary vertical.

Strengths
---------
• Massive rider network (10,000+ in Dhaka)
• Strong app UX
• Brand recognition from food delivery

Weaknesses vs JG Mart
---------------------
• Grocery is NOT their focus — restaurants are
• No grocery-specific inventory or sourcing
• High delivery fees (BDT 40-60)
• No community trust or local relationships
• Restaurant-first routing optimizations don't suit grocery batched delivery

JG Mart Counter-Strategy
------------------------
• Be the grocery specialist they can't compete with
• Emphasize fresh produce quality (their advantage over restaurant food)
• Offer lower minimums than their grocery section
• Build community before they can scale grocery

Threat Level: LOW (not a grocery-first player)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. PATHAO
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Overview
--------
Bangladesh's super app. Courier + food delivery + ride-sharing.
Strong brand, massive user base, but grocery is not a core vertical.

Strengths
---------
• Massive user base (10M+ registered)
• Courier network covers all Dhaka
• Strong brand trust
• Multi-service app (reduces churn)

Weaknesses vs JG Mart
---------------------
• Courier-first, not grocery-first
• No sourcing or inventory capability
• Higher delivery fees for non-food items
• No grocery-specific UX
• Impersonal platform; no community relationship

JG Mart Counter-Strategy
------------------------
• Own the grocery vertical in JGC before Pathao expands it
• Build deeper customer relationships via WhatsApp (not app)
• Focus on freshness and quality — Pathao's strength is logistics, not produce
• Create category expertise they can't match

Threat Level: LOW (strategic mismatch)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4. LOCAL BAZAARS & INFORMAL VENDORS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Overview
--------
Krishi Market is the primary wholesale hub. Informal delivery exists but is
unreliable. Small shops within walking distance offer limited selection.

Strengths
---------
• Trusted by community
• Cash transactions, no digital friction
• Flexible pricing
• Social relationships

Weaknesses vs JG Mart
---------------------
• No delivery to doorsteps
• Inconsistent quality and pricing
• Limited hours
• No ordering convenience
• No accountability or guarantees

JG Mart Counter-Strategy
------------------------
• Don't compete on price — compete on convenience
• Position as "bazaar quality, delivered"
• Use bazaar prices as marketing anchor
• Offer consistency bazaars can't match

Threat Level: LOW (we're complementary, not competitive)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5. FUTURE ENTRANTS / COPYCATS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Overview
--------
Any local entrepreneur seeing JG Mart's success may copy the model in JGC
or adjacent societies.

Strengths
---------

• Local knowledge
• Speed to market

Weaknesses vs JG Mart
---------------------
• No first-mover advantage
• No established partner relationships
• No customer habits locked in
• No brand trust

JG Mart Counter-Strategy
------------------------
• Move fast: lock in customers in Months 1-3
• Build exclusive partnerships with Krishi Market vendors
• Create subscription lock-in
• Establish JG Mart as "the" grocery service in JGC
• Build network effects: more customers → more volume → better prices → more customers

Threat Level: MEDIUM (defensible only if we execute fast)
