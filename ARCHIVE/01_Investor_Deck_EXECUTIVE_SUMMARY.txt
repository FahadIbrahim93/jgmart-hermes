1. THE PROBLEM
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Japan Garden City (JGC) is a densely populated residential enclave in
Mohammadpur, Dhaka, home to approximately 1,700 families. Despite this
density, the community has zero internal grocery stores. Residents must
either travel 1–2 km to the nearest bazaar or rely on informal vendors
with inconsistent pricing and quality.

For working professionals, elderly residents, and households without
private transport, daily grocery procurement represents a significant
time tax — estimated at 2–3 hours per trip, 3–4 times per week.

2. THE SOLUTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

JG Mart is a WhatsApp-based grocery delivery platform that bridges JGC
residents with Krishi Market, a wholesale fresh-produce hub located
just 800 meters from the community boundary.

• NO warehouse. NO inventory. NO capital tied up in stock.
• Customers order via WhatsApp — the app already installed on 95%+ of
  Bangladeshi smartphones.
• Orders are aggregated, sourced from Krishi Market within 2 hours,
  and delivered to doorsteps by micro-entrepreneur delivery partners.
• Average delivery time: 90 minutes from order confirmation.

Unit Economics:

  ┌─────────────────────────────┬──────────────┐

  │ Contribution Margin          │  51%         │  ← On total revenue per order
  └─────────────────────────────┴──────────────┘

NOTE: 51% contribution margin reflects variable profitability AFTER
rider, manager allocation, marketing, and packaging costs but BEFORE

flows largely to net profit.
