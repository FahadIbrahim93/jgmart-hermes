OVERVIEW — THE 7-STAGE FUNNEL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  1. AWARENESS        → They hear about JG Mart
  2. FIRST CONTACT    → They message you on WhatsApp
  3. WELCOME          → You convert the message to an order
  4. FIRST ORDER      → The experience that decides if they return
  5. FOLLOW-UP        → Post-delivery engagement
  6. SECOND ORDER     → Nudge them to buy again
  7. LOYALTY          → Referral, subscription, advocacy

KEY PRINCIPLES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. FIRST ORDER IS EVERYTHING — If the first delivery is bad, you lose them forever.
   If it's great, you have a customer for life.

2. SPEED WINS — Respond faster than competitors. In Bangladesh, WhatsApp response
   within 5 minutes is a competitive advantage.

3. PERSONALIZATION — Use their name. Remember preferences. Make them feel known,
   not like a number.

4. OVER-DELIVER ON FIRST ORDER — Free delivery is table stakes. Add something
   extra: a free lime, a handwritten thank you, a next-time discount.

5. EVERY COMPLAINT IS AN OPPORTUNITY — A well-handled complaint can create a
   more loyal customer than a perfect order.

6. DON'T SPAM — 1-2 messages per week max for engaged customers.
   Daily messages = blocked/ignored.
