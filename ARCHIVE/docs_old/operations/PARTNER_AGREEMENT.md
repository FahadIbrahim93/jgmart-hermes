# Partner Agreement

## This document is a template.

**Source:** `03_Legal_Templates/PARTNER_AGREEMENT.txt` + `JG_Mart_Partner_Agreement.docx`

## 1. Parties
- **Company:** JG Mart Hermes ("Platform")
- **Partner:** [Store Name] ("Partner")

## 2. Scope
Partner agrees to list products on the JG Mart platform, fulfill orders within 60 minutes of receipt, and maintain quality standards.

## 3. Term
- Initial term: 6 months
- Auto-renewal: Month-to-month unless 30-day notice given
- Termination: 7-day notice for cause, immediate for breach

## 4. Commission & Payouts
- Platform commission: 7% of order value
- Payout cycle: Weekly, every Monday
- Payout method: Bank transfer / UPI
- Dispute window: 48 hours post-payout

## 5. Responsibilities
- **Platform:** Catalog maintenance, rider dispatch, customer support, payment collection
- **Partner:** Inventory management, order packing, quality control, store cleanliness

## 6. SLAs
- Order fulfillment: ≤ 15 minutes from receipt
- Stock accuracy: ≥ 95% uptime on listed items
- Cancellation rate: < 5%
- Customer complaint resolution: < 2 hours

## 7. Confidentiality
Both parties agree not to disclose pricing, customer data, or operational metrics to third parties without written consent.

## 8. Indemnification
Partner indemnifies Platform against claims arising from food safety, mislabeling, or delivery failures caused by Partner negligence.

## 9. Governing Law
This agreement is governed by the laws of [State], India. Disputes resolved in [City] courts.
