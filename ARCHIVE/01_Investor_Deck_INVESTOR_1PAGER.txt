THE SOLUTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WhatsApp-native hyperlocal service.
Sourced daily from Krishi Market partners 800m away.
2-hour delivery slots. Zero warehouse. Market prices + convenience premium.

Promise: Trust • Speed • Premium Care

CONTRIBUTION / ORDER                71 BDT

NOTE: The 51% contribution margin assumes a blended subscription revenue
