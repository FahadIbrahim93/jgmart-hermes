1. BRAND STORY
================================================================================

JG Mart was born from a simple frustration: paying supermarket prices for
everyday groceries while the best produce—fresh vegetables, fruits, and
staples—sits just kilometers away at Dhaka’s Krishi Markets, sold at honest
prices by the very farmers who grew them.

Founder Fahad Ibrahim saw the gap between two worlds:
  • The premium, convenience-priced supermarkets of Gulshan and Banani.
  • The fresh, affordable, community-rooted Krishi Markets of Mohammadpur,
    Savar, and surrounding areas.

JG Mart bridges that gap. We curate the best of the Krishi Market experience
and deliver it straight to the doorsteps of Japan Garden City (JGC) and
neighboring clusters—within 2 hours. No markup drama. No middlemen fatigue.
Just fresh produce, fair prices, and the speed of modern delivery.

We exist because JGC residents deserve better grocery options without leaving
their homes, and because local farmers deserve a direct channel to urban
customers who value quality.

Our name, "JG Mart," is a nod to our home community. "Mart" signals a
neighborhood grocery—approachable, reliable, and always open when you need
it. Together, "JG Mart" means: your community, your market, your way.

2. MISSION & VISION
================================================================================

MISSION
-------
To deliver Krishi Market-quality groceries at honest prices to every home in
Japan Garden City and surrounding neighborhoods within 2 hours, every single
day—making fresh, affordable food the default choice for our community.

VISION
------
To become Dhaka’s most trusted hyperlocal grocery brand—one cluster at a
time—while uplifting local farmers and creating a scalable model that other
neighborhoods can replicate.

CORE VALUES
-----------
1. COMMUNITY FIRST   We live in the neighborhoods we serve.
2. FAIR PRICING      Krishi Market prices, not supermarket margins.
3. SPEED WITH CARE   Fast delivery that never sacrifices freshness.
4. TRANSPARENCY      Clear pricing, honest communication, no hidden fees.
5. FARMER PARTNERSHIP Direct relationships that benefit growers and eaters alike.

3. BRAND PERSONALITY
================================================================================

JG Mart is:

  TRUSTWORTHY
  -----------
  We show up on time. We price honestly. We admit mistakes and fix them fast.
  Customers should feel relaxed, not suspicious, when ordering from us.

  FAST
  ----
  "2 hours or less" is a promise, not a marketing line. Our brand moves with

  PREMIUM BUT AFFORDABLE
  ----------------------
  The quality feels premium—freshness, packaging, service—but the price stays
  rooted in Krishi Market reality. Premium experience, everyday price.

BRAND ARCHETYPE: The Helpful Neighbor
--------------------------------------
Think of that trusted local shopkeeper who always saves you the good produce,
keeps fair prices, and delivers personally when you’re busy. That’s JG Mart.

EMOTIONAL TONE
--------------
Warm, energetic, and genuinely helpful. We use humor sparingly—we’re here to
solve problems, not entertain. But when we do joke, it’s self-aware, light,
and inclusive.

4. LOGO USAGE RULES
================================================================================

4.1 CLEAR SPACE
---------------
Maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "JG"
letterform on all sides. No other graphics, text, or edges may intrude into
this zone.

  [ Clear Space = "x" where x = height of "JG" in the logo ]

4.2 MINIMUM SIZE
---------------
Digital (screen):  120px wide (minimum)
Print (CMYK):      25mm wide (minimum)

Below these sizes, use the icon-only version instead of the full wordmark.

4.3 DON’TS
----------
  ✗ Do not stretch, skew, rotate, or distort the logo.
  ✗ Do not change the logo colors outside of approved variations.
  ✗ Do not place the logo on busy backgrounds without a solid backing shape.
  ✗ Do not add drop shadows, outlines, gradients, or effects unless specified.
  ✗ Do not separate the icon from the wordmark in non-approved layouts.
  ✗ Do not use the logo in low-contrast situations where it becomes unreadable.

4.4 PLACEMENT PREFERENCE
------------------------
  • Top-left corner of digital assets and flyers.
  • Centered for primary brand hero images.
  • Bottom-right corner when overlaid on photography (keep clear space intact).

5. COLOR PALETTE
================================================================================

PRIMARY GREEN
-------------
Hex:  #059669  (Deep Emerald — trust, stability, growth)
RGB:  5, 150, 105
CMYK: 78, 0, 65, 28
Usage: Primary brand color. Headlines, buttons, key accents.

SECONDARY GREEN
---------------
Hex:  #10B981  (Bright Mint — freshness, speed, energy)
RGB:  16, 185, 129
CMYK: 72, 0, 60, 0
Usage: Highlights, hover states, secondary buttons, success indicators.

WHITE
-----
Hex:  #FFFFFF
RGB:  255, 255, 255
CMYK: 0, 0, 0, 0
Usage: Backgrounds, negative space, text on dark backgrounds.

TEXT COLORS
-----------
Primary Text:   #1F2937  (Dark Gray — almost black, softer than pure black)
Secondary Text: #6B7280  (Medium Gray — captions, subtitles)
Accent Text:    #059669  (Primary Green for links and emphasis)

BACKGROUND COLORS
-----------------
Primary BG:     #FFFFFF  (White)
Secondary BG:   #ECFDF5  (Very light mint tint for cards/sections)
Dark BG:        #064E3B  (Deep green for footers, dark headers)

ACCESSIBILITY CHECK
-------------------
Primary green on white meets WCAG AA for large text. For small text or UI
buttons, use #059669 text on white or white text on #059669 background for
optimal contrast.

6. TYPOGRAPHY GUIDELINES
================================================================================

ENGLISH TYPOGRAPHY
------------------
Primary Font Family:  Inter (Sans-serif)
Secondary Font Family: Poppins (Headlines & display text)
Monospace (for prices/codes): DM Mono

Font Hierarchy:
  H1 (Hero / Main Headlines):    Poppins Bold / 600   — 36–48px
  H2 (Section Headlines):        Poppins SemiBold / 500 — 28–36px
  H3 (Sub-sections):             Poppins Medium / 500   — 22–28px
  Body (Paragraphs):             Inter Regular / 400    — 16–18px
  Small / Captions:              Inter Medium / 500     — 12–14px
  Buttons / Labels:              Inter SemiBold / 600   — 14–16px

BANGLA TYPEOGRAPHY
------------------
Primary Font Family:  Noto Sans Bengali (Kalpurush if Noto unavailable)
Secondary Font Family: Noto Serif Bengali (for formal/headline use)

Font Hierarchy:
  H1 (Hero / Main Headlines):    Noto Sans Bengali Bold  — 32–42px
  H2 (Section Headlines):        Noto Sans Bengali SemiBold — 26–32px
  H3 (Sub-sections):             Noto Sans Bengali Medium — 20–24px
  Body (Paragraphs):             Noto Sans Bengali Regular — 16–18px
  Small / Captions:              Noto Sans Bengali Medium — 12–14px

TYPESETTING RULES
-----------------
  • Line height: 1.5× for body text, 1.2× for headlines.
  • Paragraph spacing: 1.0× font size between paragraphs.
  • Maximum line length: 70 characters for readability.
  • Always use Bangla numerals (১২, ৩৪) for Bangla text. Use Western numerals
    (1, 2, 3) for English text and mixed content.
  • Avoid all-caps for long passages. All-caps acceptable for short labels
    only, with increased letter-spacing (10–20%).

7. VOICE & TONE
================================================================================

JG Mart speaks like a friendly, knowledgeable neighbor who genuinely wants to
help. We are never corporate, never robotic, and never pushy.

ATTRIBUTES
----------
  Clear        We explain things simply. No jargon.
  Friendly     Warm without being cheesy.
  Helpful      Every message offers value or solves a problem.
  Confident    We know our quality and prices are good—no need to shout.
  Inclusive    We speak to students, families, and elderly residents equally.

CHANNEL-SPECIFIC GUIDANCE
--------------------------

WHATSAPP MESSAGES
  • Short, scannable lines (1–2 sentences max per bullet).
  • Use emojis sparingly and meaningfully (🥬, 🚚, ⏰, 💚).
  • Always include a clear call-to-action (CTA).
  • Format with line breaks and bullet points, not walls of text.
  Example:
    🥬 Fresh Morning Catalog — July 28
    Today’s picks: Tomatoes ৳40/kg, Potatoes ৳25/kg
    Order by 9AM for delivery by 11AM!
    Reply or tap: bit.ly/jgmart-order

SOCIAL MEDIA POSTS (Instagram / Facebook)
  • Hook in the first line. Make it visual or relatable.
  • Mix Bangla and English naturally (code-switching is expected and preferred).
  • Keep captions under 220 words; put longer stories in comments or Stories.
  • Use #JG Mart, #JGC, #DhakaDelivery, #KrishiMarket hashtags.
  • Tag @JGC_Mart and encourage tagging friends who shop at JGC.

FLYERS & PRINT
  • Headline-first. The reader should grasp the offer in 3 seconds.
  • Use bullet points, not paragraphs, for benefits.
  • Bold the price. Make the CTA phone number or QR code impossible to miss.
  • Keep Bangla text slightly larger than English for dominant readability.

EMAIL / NEWSLETTER
  • Subject lines under 50 characters for mobile.
  • Personal greeting using the customer’s first name when possible.
  • Single-column layout, one primary CTA per section.
  • "View in browser" link always included.

8. PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE
================================================================================

PHILOSOPHY
----------
Authentic, fresh, and rooted in the Krishi Market reality. We do not use
overly stylized, sterile supermarket stock photos. Our imagery should feel
like a neighbor’s kitchen—bright, real, and appetizing.

LIGHTING
--------
  • Bright, natural daylight. Avoid harsh flash.
  • Soft shadows. Food should glow, not glare.
  • Morning light preferred (matches our morning delivery promise).

COMPOSITION
-----------
  • Close-up textures: dew on cucumbers, rough potato skin, vibrant red
    tomato slices.
  • Loose, generous negative space around subjects for social media crops.
  • People shots: real JGC residents (with consent), delivery riders in
    uniform, families cooking together.

COLOR GRADING
-------------
  • Boost greens and warm tones slightly.
  • Keep whites clean—no yellow/sepia tints unless for a specific campaign.
  • Maintain natural skin tones. Avoid over-saturation.

SUBJECTS
--------
  • Fresh produce at Krishi Market stalls (crates, baskets, heaped piles).
  • JG Mart branded delivery bags on doorsteps, balconies, and elevators.
  • Product-only shots for catalog: top-down or 45-degree angle, white or
    light wooden background.
  • Behind-the-scenes: packing, sorting, loading, riding.

STYLE DON’TS
------------
  ✗ Over-edited, plastic-looking food.
  ✗ Generic stock models who do not look like JGC residents.
  ✗ Dark, moody food photography (we are bright and fresh, not gourmet-fine).
  ✗ Excessive text overlays on photography (let the image breathe).

9. DO’S AND DON’TS
================================================================================

DO
--
  ✔ Use our green palette consistently across all touchpoints.
  ✔ Keep messaging simple, direct, and benefit-oriented.
  ✔ Show real people, real products, real JGC locations.
  ✔ Highlight speed ("2 hours") and price ("Krishi Market prices") together.
  ✔ Use Bangla and English together—it is our community’s natural voice.
  ✔ Maintain clear logo spacing on every piece of creative.
  ✔ Update seasonal catalogs weekly to keep content fresh.
  ✔ Test QR codes and links before printing flyers.
  ✔ Respond to negative comments publicly with empathy and a solution.
  ✔ Celebrate local JGC milestones (block tournaments, festivals, etc.).

DON’T
-----
  ✗ Use pure black (#000000) for body text—use #1F2937 instead.
  ✗ Promise "1 hour" delivery unless we can guarantee it 99% of the time.
  ✗ Post low-resolution images that look pixelated on mobile.
  ✗ Use overly formal or corporate language ("leverage synergies," etc.).
  ✗ Run flash sales without restocking—nothing damages trust faster than
    an advertised product that is immediately out of stock.
  ✗ Share customer photos or names without explicit permission.
  ✗ Ignore Ramadan, Eid, or local holiday timing in scheduling posts.
  ✗ Compare ourselves negatively to competitors—focus on our own strengths.
  ✗ Use confusing bundle pricing—always show per-unit and total clearly.
  ✗ Leave outdated flyers or posters up after promotions end.
