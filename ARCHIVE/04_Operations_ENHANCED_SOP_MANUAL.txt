SECTION 2: ORDER PROCESSING STEP-BY-STEP
================================================================================

1. INTAKE
   - Customer sends WhatsApp message: "I want [items] + delivery to [address]"
   - Ops Lead logs order in Google Sheets / Order Tracker with timestamp
   - Order ID format: JG-[Date]-[Sequential] (e.g., JG-280726-001)

2. VALIDATION
   - Confirm address belongs to serviceable area (4 clusters)
   - Verify stock availability with partner
   - Calculate total (items + delivery fee)
   - Send WhatsApp confirmation with exact total + payment method

3. PAYMENT CONFIRMATION
   - COD: Customer pays rider upon delivery
   - bKash: Customer sends screenshot OR Ops Lead confirms via bKash statement
   - Advance payment: Process before dispatch

4. PICKING
   - Ops Lead assigns order to partner (P001–P005)
   - Partner prepares items, tags with Order ID
   - Rider collects batch, scans for completeness

5. DISPATCH & DELIVERY
   - Rider follows optimized cluster route
   - Sends pre-arrival WhatsApp message (5 mins before)
   - Delivers, collects payment, obtains signature/WhatsApp acknowledgement

6. POST-DELIVERY
   - Mark order "Delivered" in tracker
   - Send thank-you WhatsApp with feedback request
   - Log any issues (wrong item, damaged, late)

7. CANCELLATION / RETURN
   - Customer must request within 10 mins of ordering
   - If rider already dispatched, cancellation fee = delivery fee
   - Returned items logged, credit issued within 24 hours

SECTION 6: QUALITY CONTROL
================================================================================

QC CHECKLIST (Before Dispatch)
  [ ] All items match order slip
  [ ] Perishables within expiry date (check labels)
  [ ] Packaging intact (no leaks, tears)
  [ ] Temperature-sensitive items in cool bag
  [ ] Heavy items double-bagged
  [ ] Order tag visible and correct

INCOMING QC (Partner to Hub)
  [ ] Spot-check 10% of incoming orders for correct items
  [ ] Photograph any discrepancies immediately
  [ ] Log in QC Daily Sheet with timestamp + photo

OUTGOING QC (Hub to Customer)
  [ ] Rider checks order completeness before leaving hub
  [ ] Customer signs / WhatsApp acknowledges receipt
  [ ] If customer absent, photograph left-at-door placement + send WhatsApp

RETURNS & DAMAGES
  - Customer returns within 15 mins of delivery: full refund, no questions
  - Returns after 15 mins: case-by-case basis, photo evidence required
  - Damaged in transit: rider liable if negligence proven; company absorbs if packaging failure

PHOTO QC REQUIREMENTS
  - Missing/broken item: photo + timestamp before refund
  - Wrong address delivery: photo of correct location + rider GPS screenshot
  - Perishable spoilage: photo of item + expiry date before replacement

SECTION 7: EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS
================================================================================

BIKE BREAKDOWN
  1. Rider calls Ops Lead immediately
  2. Ops Lead arranges backup bike or transfers orders to other rider
  3. Customer notified within 10 mins with revised ETA
  4. Bike repaired within 4 hours or备用方案 activated

CUSTOMER NOT HOME
  1. Call customer (2 rings max)
  2. If no answer, WhatsApp message + photo at door
  3. Return to hub if no response within 15 mins
  4. Redeliver next day (no extra charge) or arrange pickup point

ACCIDENT / INJURY
  1. Ensure rider safety first; call emergency services if needed
  2. Call Founder + Ops Lead immediately
  3. Document scene with photos
  4. Inform customer of delay, arrange redelivery or refund
  5. Insurance claim within 24 hours

SEVERE WEATHER (see also Section 10: Monsoon Playbook)
  1. Founder declares weather hold if rain intensity > threshold
  2. WhatsApp broadcast to all pending customers
  3. Reschedule to next available slot or full refund

SECTION 10: BKASH RECONCILIATION PROCESS
================================================================================

DAILY BKASH RECONCILIATION (18:00 – 19:00)
  1. Ops Lead downloads bKash statement for the day (PDF / app screenshot)
  2. Cross-reference each bKash transaction with Order Tracker:
     - Match: bKash TrxID ↔ Order ID ↔ Customer name ↔ Amount
     - Flag: Unmatched bKash receipts for investigation
     - Flag: Orders marked "paid" without bKash confirmation
  3. Reconciliation sheet columns:
     Date | Order ID | Customer | Expected Amount | bKash TrxID | Sender Number | Status (Matched/Unmatched/Short/Excess) | Notes

COMMON DISCREPANCIES & FIXES
  - Wrong amount sent: Customer refunds difference or Ops Lead adjusts order total
  - Duplicate TrxID: Confirm with bKash support, refund duplicate
  - Missing TrxID: Call customer, request screenshot, match within 24 hours
  - Wrong sender number: Verify with customer, update records

WEEKLY BKASH AUDIT (Sunday 18:00)
  1. Total bKash receipts vs. Total bKash orders (by count and value)
  2. Identify patterns: specific customers, specific rider, specific time windows
  3. Adjust rider cash float or bKash collection SOP if anomaly persists
  4. Report to Founder: Net bKash collected, fees, discrepancies resolved

BKASH FEES ACCOUNTING
  - bKash fee per transaction: BDT [X] (flat or percentage – update current rate)
  - Deduct from daily settlement or absorb as operational cost
  - Record in Financial Log under "Payment Gateway Fees"

SECTION 11: END-OF-DAY CASH HANDLING PROCEDURE
================================================================================

CASH COLLECTION SUMMARY (16:30 – 17:30)
  - Rider counts cash in presence of Ops Lead (two-person rule)
  - Count denominations separately: BDT 1000, 500, 200, 100, 50, 20, 10
  - Verify against Delivery Log:
    Total Expected = Sum of all COD orders for the day
    Total Collected = Cash on hand
    Variance = Collected – Expected
    Shortage / Excess = Variance (must be explained)

CASH DEPOSIT / SAFEKEEPING
  1. Cash counted and sealed in tamper-evident envelope
  2. Envelope signed by Rider + Ops Lead with date + amount
  3. Deposit into lockable safe / bank deposit box at hub
  4. Safe combination known only to Founder + Ops Lead
  5. Bank deposit next business day (or daily if > BDT 50,000)

CASH FLOAT MANAGEMENT
  - Morning float: BDT 5,000 (replenished from previous day's deposit)
  - Maximum float on rider: BDT 15,000 (for high-value COD orders)
  - If float runs low midday, Ops Lead delivers top-up to rider

CASH SHORTAGE PROTOCOL
  - Shortage < BDT 500: Rider explains, Ops Lead approves write-off or deduction
  - Shortage BDT 500–2,000: Written explanation required, 50% deduction from next payout
  - Shortage > BDT 2,000: Investigation by Founder, possible police report, rider suspended pending investigation

CASH SECURITY RULES
  - Never leave cash unattended in bike or unlocked hub
  - If rider feels unsafe, deposit cash at nearest trusted shop (record location)
  - All cash transactions require receipt (pre-printed or handwritten with Order ID)
