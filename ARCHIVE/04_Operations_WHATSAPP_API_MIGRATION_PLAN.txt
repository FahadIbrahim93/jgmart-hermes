1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

JG Mart currently runs its entire WhatsApp communication on the free WhatsApp
Business App installed on a personal phone with a single SIM (+8801870489448).
There is no backup phone, no backup SIM, and no protection against a ban.

  RISK: If WhatsApp bans the number — for sending too many broadcast messages,
  rapid replies, or receiving user reports — the entire business goes dark.
  Customer orders, supplier communication, and delivery coordination stop
  immediately. Number recovery from a ban is nearly impossible.

  SOLUTION: Migrate to the official WhatsApp Business API (Cloud API or via a
  BSP). This is the same infrastructure used by Daraz, Foodpanda, and bKash.
  Messages are sent through Meta's servers with proper rate limits, template
  pre-approval, and no ban risk for legitimate business use.

  TIMELINE: 3–4 weeks total.
  COST (estimated): BDT 2,000–5,000/month for 1,000 conversations.
  FREE TIER AVAILABLE: WhatsApp Cloud API (Meta-hosted) is free for the first

2. WHY THE WHATSAPP BUSINESS APP IS A BUSINESS RISK
================================================================================

2.1 Volume Triggers (Spam Detection)
  - WhatsApp's algorithm flags numbers sending more than ~250–500 messages/day
    as potential spam.
  - JG Mart sends daily broadcast messages (today's offers, new arrivals),
    rapid order confirmations, and delivery updates — easily exceeding these
    thresholds on busy days.
  - The Business App was designed for small shops with 10–50 customers/day,
    not a growing delivery business.

2.2 Customer Reports
  - If any customer marks a message as "Spam" or reports the number, WhatsApp
    reviews the account. Even one report can trigger a review.
  - In a delivery business, customer frustration (late delivery, missing item)
    can lead to reports. You have no appeal process on the Business App.

2.3 No Multi-Device Support
  - Only one phone can operate the WhatsApp Business number.
  - If the phone is lost, stolen, or damaged, you cannot message customers
    until you get a new SIM + new WhatsApp setup.
  - No team inbox — multiple staff cannot handle customer chats simultaneously.

2.4 No Template Pre-Approval
  - The Business App sends messages freely, which means WhatsApp thinks you
    might be sending spam. The API requires pre-approved message templates,
    which guarantees deliverability.

2.5 No Backup, No Recovery
  - Current setup: one SIM card, one phone. If the SIM is damaged, lost, or
    the number is banned, zero recourse.
  - WhatsApp does not restore banned Business App numbers. You lose your
    customer chat history and the number itself.

3. WHAT IS THE WHATSAPP BUSINESS API?
================================================================================

The WhatsApp Business API (also called the WhatsApp Business Platform) is the
enterprise-grade version of WhatsApp. It is the same technology used by:

  - bKash (payment notifications, OTPs)
  - Daraz (order confirmations, delivery tracking)
  - Foodpanda (order updates, rider coordination)
  - Bank Asia, Dutch-Bangla Bank (transaction alerts)

3.1 How It Differs from the Business App

  Feature               | Business App          | Business API
  -----------------------+-----------------------+------------------------------
  Number risk           | High (ban-prone)      | Very low (compliant usage)
  Multi-agent inbox     | No                    | Yes (team of 10+ agents)
  Broadcasts            | Manual, risky         | Scheduled, template-approved
  Auto-replies          | Basic                 | Advanced (chatbot, rules)
  Customer history      | On one phone          | Cloud-based, permanent
  Rate limits           | Low (~250/day)        | High (10,000+/day)
  Analytics             | None                  | Delivery rates, read rates
  Cost                  | Free                  | Per-message + platform fee
  Backup                | None                  | Built-in (cloud)
  Integration           | No                    | APIs for CRM/ERP/website

3.2 Message Categories & Pricing

  Since July 2025, Meta charges per delivered template message (not per
  conversation). The four categories are:

  CATEGORY       | WHEN CHARGED           | BANGLADESH RATE (USD)
  ---------------+------------------------+-----------------------
  Marketing      | Always charged         | $0.0732 per message
  Utility        | Charged only OUTSIDE   | $0.0113 per message
                 | the 24h service window |
  Authentication | Always charged         | $0.0113 per message
  Service        | Always free            | Free (within 24h window)

  KEY OPTIMISATION: If a customer messages you, a 24-hour "service window"
  opens. Any utility template (order confirmation, delivery update) sent
  inside that window is FREE. Marketing templates are always charged.

5. BSP (BUSINESS SOLUTION PROVIDER) OPTIONS FOR BANGLADESH
================================================================================

Meta does not sell the WhatsApp API directly to most businesses. You need a
BSP (Business Solution Provider). Here are the options that work in Bangladesh:

5.1 RECOMMENDED: Whatsfly (Local Bangladesh Provider)
  - Website: https://whatsfly.net
  - Pricing: Starts ~$30/month flat, no markup on Meta template fees
  - Pros: Local support team in Bangladesh, helps with business verification,
          6+ team seats included, flat transparent pricing
  - Cons: Smaller than global providers, fewer integrations
  - Best for: Bangladeshi SMBs that want local support

5.2 WhatsApp Cloud API (Meta-Hosted — Free Tier Available)
  - Sign up directly at: https://developers.facebook.com (WhatsApp → Cloud API)
  - Pricing: FREE for first 1,000 conversations/month.
             After that, standard per-message rates apply.
  - Pros: No BSP markup, free tier, direct Meta support, no monthly fee
  - Cons: Requires technical setup (webhooks, API calls). No GUI dashboard.
           You need a developer to set it up initially.
  - Best for: Tech-savvy teams wanting lowest cost
  - RECOMMENDED for Phase 1 (start free, scale later)

5.3 Twilio
  - Pricing: Meta rates + $0.005/message markup. No monthly platform fee.
  - Pros: Rock-solid infrastructure, excellent documentation, global support
  - Cons: More technical. No chat dashboard — you build your own.
           $0.005/message markup adds up.
  - Best for: Businesses with a developer who wants full control

5.4 WATI
  - Website: https://www.wati.io
  - Pricing: From ~$30/month (includes some conversation credits)
  - Pros: Great CRM integrations (Zoho, HubSpot, Shopify), easy-to-use GUI,
          built-in broadcast tools, team inbox
  - Cons: Can get expensive at scale, mostly India-focused support
  - Best for: Non-technical teams wanting a ready-to-use dashboard

5.5 Interakt
  - Website: https://interakt.co
  - Pricing: From ~₹1,000-₹15,000/month (~BDT 150-2,200/month)
  - Pros: Indian company, good for South Asia, easy setup
  - Cons: INR pricing adds FX complexity for Bangladesh
  - Best for: Budget-conscious businesses

5.6 Gupshup
  - Website: https://www.gupshup.io
  - Pricing: Platform fee + Meta rates. Competitive for high volume.
  - Pros: Strong in South Asia, good API docs, chatbot builder included
  - Cons: Complex pricing, sales-driven
  - Best for: Businesses planning heavy automation

5.7 Comparison Table

  BSP          | Monthly Fee | Markup on Meta | Free Tier | Ease of Setup
  -------------+------------+----------------+-----------+--------------
  Cloud API    | $0         | None           | 1K free   | Technical
  Whatsfly     | ~$30       | None           | No        | Easy (local)
  Twilio       | $0         | $0.005/msg     | No        | Technical
  WATI         | ~$30       | Varies         | Trial     | Easy (GUI)
  Interakt     | ~$5-30     | Varies         | Trial     | Easy (GUI)

6. MIGRATION PHASES & TIMELINE
================================================================================

  TOTAL: 3–4 Weeks (assuming documents are ready)

  PHASE 1: Set Up BSP Account + Business Verification
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Duration: 1–2 weeks
  Goal: Get a verified WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) on the API

  Steps:
  [ ] 1. Choose BSP (recommended: Whatsfly for local support OR Cloud API for
         free tier).
  [ ] 2. Create a Meta Business Account at business.facebook.com.
  [ ] 3. Submit business verification documents to Meta:
         - Trade license (trade license copy, English/Bangla)
         - TIN certificate
         - Bank statement (last 3 months)
         - Business address proof (utility bill or rental agreement)
         - Valid phone number for verification (can use a different number)
  [ ] 4. Wait for Meta verification (typically 3–10 business days).
  [ ] 5. Once verified, set up WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) and
         configure the phone number.
  [ ] 6. Choose: Use existing number (+8801870489448) OR a new number.

  IMPORTANT DECISION: Using the existing number requires a 30-day cool-down
  period after unregistering from WhatsApp Business App before it can be
  registered on the API. If you cannot go 30 days without WhatsApp, get a
  SECOND number (see Phase 4).

  PHASE 2: Configure Message Templates
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Duration: 3–5 days (parallel with Phase 1)
  Goal: Get pre-approved message templates for your use cases

  Required templates for JG Mart:

  a) ORDER CONFIRMATION (Utility)
     Template: "Hi {{1}}, your order #{{2}} has been confirmed. 
     Total: BDT {{3}}. We'll deliver to {{4}} within {{5}} minutes.
     Track: {{6}}"
     Category: Utility (cheaper)

  b) DELIVERY UPDATE (Utility)
     Template: "Hi {{1}}, your order #{{2}} is out for delivery! 
     Rider {{3}} is on the way. ETA: {{4}} minutes."
     Category: Utility

  c) DAILY OFFERS (Marketing)
     Template: "🛒 Today's Special at JG Mart! {{1}}
     Order now: {{2}} | Free delivery on orders over BDT {{3}}"
     Category: Marketing

  d) ORDER READY FOR PICKUP (Utility)
     Template: "Hi {{1}}, your order #{{2}} is ready for pickup at
     {{3}}. Please collect within {{4}} minutes."
     Category: Utility

  e) PAYMENT RECEIPT (Utility)
     Template: "Hi {{1}}, payment of BDT {{2}} for order #{{3}} is
     confirmed. Thank you for shopping at JG Mart!"
     Category: Utility

  f) CUSTOMER SUPPORT ACKNOWLEDGEMENT (Service — no template needed)
     Free-form replies within the 24-hour service window.

  Template approval takes 24–48 hours per template after submission.

  PHASE 3: Migrate Customer Base
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Duration: 2–3 days
  Goal: Export customer contacts from phone and import to BSP platform

  Steps:
  [ ] 1. Export WhatsApp Business App contacts (Android: Settings > Chats >
         Chat backup or use a third-party export tool).
  [ ] 2. Clean and deduplicate the contact list.
  [ ] 3. Segment customers into groups:
         - Active customers (ordered in last 30 days)
         - Regular customers (ordered in last 90 days)
         - Inactive / new leads
  [ ] 4. Import contacts to BSP platform.
  [ ] 5. Tag customers for targeted broadcasts (e.g., "Vegetarian",
         "Dairy-heavy", "Evening delivery").

  IMPORTANT: You need opt-in/opt-out consent for WhatsApp API messaging.
  Existing customers who have messaged you on WhatsApp already have
  implied consent. Add a welcome message flow for new customers.

  PHASE 4: Switch to API (Number Strategy)
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Duration: 1–2 days
  Goal: Go live on the API without losing customer communication

  OPTION A — Keep Existing Number (+8801870489448)
    1. Unregister the number from WhatsApp Business App.
       Settings > Account > Delete My Account.
    2. Wait 30 days (Meta's cool-down period).
    3. Register the number on the API through your BSP.
    4. During the 30 days, use a temporary phone/SIM with a broadcast
       message to customers: "JG Mart is upgrading! Our new temporary
       number for orders is +8801XXX... Please save it."

  OPTION B — Get a New Number (RECOMMENDED)
    1. Get a second SIM (any Bangladeshi operator — GP, Robi, Banglalink,
       or Teletalk).
    2. Register the NEW number on the API.
    3. Broadcast from the old Business App: "JG Mart is on a new number!
       Save +8801XXX... for all orders and support."
    4. Run both numbers in parallel for 2–4 weeks.
    5. Gradually phase out the old number.
    6. Keep the old number active as a backup (see Section 8).

  RECOMMENDED: Option B (new number). It avoids the 30-day downtime and
  gives you a clean start on the API with zero ban risk on the new number.

  PHASE 5: Set Up Automation
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Duration: 3–5 days
  Goal: Configure auto-replies, quick replies, and broadcast lists

  [ ] 1. Set up auto-reply for order confirmation (triggered when a
         customer sends an order via WhatsApp).
  [ ] 2. Configure quick replies for common questions:
         - "What's today's special?" → Daily menu card
         - "Delivery time?" → Standard delivery hours
         - "Minimum order?" → Minimum order amount
         - "My order status" → Order tracking link
  [ ] 3. Set up away message (for after-hours).
  [ ] 4. Configure broadcast lists:
         - Morning broadcast (offers + daily menu): 8 AM daily
         - Lunch reminder: 11 AM
         - Evening restock alert: 5 PM
  [ ] 5. Assign team members to the inbox (if using a BSP with GUI).
  [ ] 6. Set up order tagging system in the BSP for tracking.

8. BACKUP NUMBER STRATEGY
================================================================================

This is critical. After migrating to the API, you MUST maintain a backup.

8.1 Recommended Setup

  ACTIVE LINE:  +8801870489448 (or the new API number)
  BACKUP LINE:  A second SIM in a separate phone

8.2 Backup Phone Requirements
  - A cheap Android phone (BDT 3,000–5,000 is enough)
  - A second SIM from a DIFFERENT operator than your main line
    (e.g., if main is GP, backup is Robi or Banglalink)
  - WhatsApp Business App installed and logged into the API phone number
    (WhatsApp allows one API number + one linked device)
  - Keep the backup phone charged and connected to WiFi at the store

8.3 What to Do If the Primary Number Goes Down
  1. If banned: You still have the backup number.
  2. Announce on backup number: "JG Mart is temporarily on this number."
  3. Contact BSP support to appeal the ban.
  4. If unrecoverable, the backup number becomes the new primary.
  5. Get a third SIM + phone as the new backup.

8.4 Never Run on a Single SIM Again
  - Single point of failure = single SIM. This is how JG Mart operates
    today and it is unacceptable for a growing business.
  - Cost of backup: ~BDT 500/month (SIM + minimal phone)
  - Cost of downtime: Thousands in lost orders + customer trust

10. FREE ALTERNATIVES: WHATSAPP CLOUD API (META-HOSTED)
================================================================================

Meta offers a free, self-serve option called the WhatsApp Cloud API. This is
the BEST starting point for JG Mart.

10.1 What's Free
  - First 1,000 conversations per month (all categories combined).
  - No BSP markup — you pay only Meta's per-message rate after 1,000.
  - No monthly platform fee.
  - Free test numbers for development.
  - No minimum commitment.

10.2 What You Need
  - Technical setup (someone to configure webhooks, API calls, and a simple
    dashboard — or use a no-code tool like n8n + Cloud API).
  - A Meta Business Account.
  - Business verification (same documents as Section 7).

10.3 Recommended Path for JG Mart

  PHASE 1 (Month 1-3): Start with Cloud API
    Cost: $0/month platform fee.
    Volume: ~500-800 conversations/month → stays within free tier.
    Setup: Use n8n (self-hosted, $0) or a simple Python script to handle
    incoming webhooks and send template messages.
    If you don't have a developer: Use Whatsfly or WATI instead (easier).

  PHASE 2 (Month 4+): If volume exceeds 1,000 conversations/month
    Cost: ~BDT 2,500-5,000/month.
    Option A: Continue on Cloud API (pay per-message after free tier).
    Option B: Switch to a BSP with GUI dashboard for team use.

  Cloud API Sign-Up URL:
    https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/get-started
