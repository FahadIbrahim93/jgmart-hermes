  • Building 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 22, 23, 25

  • Building 4, 8, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17, 19, 21, 24, 26
  • May require internal navigation. Sometimes crowded. Lift delays possible.
  • Can do 3-4 deliveries per hour.

★★★ HARD (Target: 30+ min per delivery):
  • Currently none classified as hard, but watch for:

    — Peak hours (8-9 AM, 1-2 PM, 7-8 PM) — lifts are slow
    — Friday noon-2 PM — reduced staff, harder to find people
    — Special events at club/mosque — blocked roads

ADDRESS FORMAT — STANDARDIZE FOR JG MART
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

To avoid confusion, standardize how customers give their address:

  Required format:
  "Building [Number], Flat [Number], [Optional: Floor]"

  Examples:
  ✅ "Building 7, Flat 4B"
  ✅ "Building 12, Flat 3, 3rd floor"
  ✅ "Building 21, Flat 1A (ground floor)"
  ❌ "Opposite the club" (ambiguous)
  ❌ "Near the mosque" (which mosque? which side?)
  ❌ "White building" (too vague)

  Ask customers:
  "Which building number? And which flat? Flat number helps the delivery person
   find you faster."

SPECIAL DELIVERY INSTRUCTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Common delivery notes you'll encounter:

  🐕 Dogs/Pets:    Some flats have dogs. Knock loudly, call out, wait for owner.
                   Don't leave package outside if there's a dog.

  👶 Children:     If children open the door, ask them to call an adult. Don't
                   leave items with minors unsupervised.

  🔔 No response:  If no one answers:
                   1. Call the customer
                   2. Wait 5 minutes
                   3. Try calling again
                   4. If still no answer, ask where to leave (security, neighbor)
                   5. Take a photo of where you left it
                   6. Message the customer with photo + location

  🅿️ Parking:      If parking near delivery building is full:
                   1. Find nearest legal parking spot
                   2. Lock bike securely
                   3. Take the items and walk
                   4. Don't leave bike unlocked EVER

  🌧️ Rain:         Use insulated bag covers. Keep electronics dry. Drive slower.
                   Add 10-15 min to delivery time estimates.

  🌙 Night delivery (after 7 PM):  Use phone flashlight. Be extra careful.
                    Some internal roads may be dark. Carry a small flashlight.

RIDER ROUTES — EFFICIENT DELIVERY PATTERNS

Optimize your delivery route by cluster:

  ROUTE 1 — MORNING CLUSTER (Fast, Light):
    Start: Krishi Market (7:30 AM)
    → Cluster A (5 deliveries)
    → Cluster B (3 deliveries)
    → Back to base / next pickup
    Est. time: 60-75 min

  ROUTE 2 — CENTRAL ROUND (Standard):
    Start: Pickup point
    → Cluster B (5 deliveries)
    → Cluster D (4 deliveries)
    → Cluster C (3 deliveries)
    Est. time: 90-110 min

  ROUTE 3 — SOUTHERN EXPANSE (Longer):
    Start: Pickup point
    → Cluster C (4 deliveries)
    → Cluster E (5 deliveries)
    → Back to base
    Est. time: 100-120 min

  ROUTE 4 — FULL JGC (Maximum deliveries):
    Cluster A (3) → B (3) → D (3) → C (2) → E (3)
    Est. time: 150-180 min (full round)

  TIP: Group deliveries by CLUSTER. Don't bounce between far clusters — it wastes
  time. Fill one cluster before moving to the next.
