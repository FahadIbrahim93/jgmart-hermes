Goal: Soft launch inside Japan Garden City, prove unit economics, build 80–120 
repeat customers, and prepare for full operations.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (Days 1–7)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Day 1–2
□ Print all checklists, scorecards, training card, partner guide
□ Finalize 3–4 core partners using Negotiation Scorecard
□ Sign simple agreements / WhatsApp confirmations
□ Load first inventory float (non-perishables + key staples)

Day 3–4
□ Set up WhatsApp Business catalog (basic)
□ Create 3–5 sample order templates
□ Train Brother on Pickup Checklist + Rider Training Card
□ Dry run: one full pickup → sort → mock delivery

Day 5–7
□ Soft announce to 30–50 known families / building groups
□ Offer free delivery + small welcome credit on first order
□ Process first 10–15 real orders
□ Daily review of every order (quality, timing, margin)

Success Metric Week 1: 15+ completed orders, zero major quality complaints

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PHASE 2: LEARNING & STABILISATION (Days 8–21)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

□ Increase daily order target gradually (aim 8–15 orders/day by Day 21)
□ Track every order in simple sheet (AOV, contribution, issues)
□ Hold 15-minute daily stand-up (Fahad + Brother)
□ Review partner performance every Sunday using Tracker
□ Collect customer feedback after every 3rd order
□ Fix top 3 recurring issues within 48 hours
□ Start simple referral offer (100 BDT credit)

Success Metric Week 3: 60–80 total orders, >40% repeat rate, contribution margin ≥45%

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PHASE 3: PROVE & PREPARE (Days 22–30)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

□ Push for 12–20 orders/day
□ Document all SOPs that actually worked (update manuals)
□ Calculate real unit economics from pilot data
□ Decide: continue / adjust pricing / drop weak partners
□ Prepare short pilot report (orders, AOV, margin, top issues, learnings)
□ Plan next 60 days (more buildings, possible second rider, catalog expansion)

Success Metric Day 30: 
• 120+ cumulative orders
• Clear proof of positive contribution margin
• 2–3 reliable partners locked
• Rider process running without daily firefighting

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
DAILY NON-NEGOTIABLES DURING PILOT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Every order uses Pickup Checklist
2. Every partner interaction logged (even briefly)
3. Cash reconciled same day
4. One improvement noted every evening
5. Customer complaints answered within 1 hour

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
KILL CRITERIA (Stop or Pivot If)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

• Contribution margin stays below 30% after 20 orders
• Quality complaints >15% of orders
• Cannot find 2 reliable partners after 10 days
• Rider cannot complete pickup + delivery cycle consistently
