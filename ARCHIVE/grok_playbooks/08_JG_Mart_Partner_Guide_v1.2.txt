PART 3: SUPPLIER NEGOTIATION PLAYBOOK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This section is for the JG Mart founder/ops team when negotiating with new or existing suppliers at Krishi Market or nearby wholesale points.

A. PREPARATION BEFORE MEETING
□ Research current wholesale prices for the top 10–15 SKUs you need
□ Know your target margin (aim for 10–15% contribution after all costs)
□ Prepare a simple one-page partner benefit sheet (volume guarantee, weekly payment, zero marketing cost for them)
□ Decide your walk-away price for each major category

B. NEGOTIATION STRATEGY (Bangladesh Market Context)
1. Start with relationship – talk about consistency and volume first, price second.
2. Offer volume commitment: “If quality is good we will take X kg/day regularly.”
3. Ask for best wholesale rate first, then discuss commission structure.
4. Use comparative leverage carefully: “Other suppliers are offering X – can you match or improve?”
5. Bundle categories: negotiate better rates on vegetables if they also supply rice/oil.
6. Secure payment terms: prefer weekly settlement (builds trust) over daily.
7. Always leave the door open: “Let’s start with a 15-day trial and review.”

C. KEY TALKING POINTS TO USE
• “We only work with 4–6 trusted partners. You will get steady daily volume.”
• “Payment every Tuesday without fail – no excuses.”
• “We handle all customer complaints. You just focus on quality supply.”
• “If you give us better rates, we can feature you as preferred partner and increase volume.”

D. WHAT TO LOCK IN WRITING
□ Exact wholesale price list (or formula) for main items
□ Commission percentage or fixed margin
□ Daily/weekly volume expectation (minimum and maximum)
□ Quality standards (especially for fish, meat, vegetables)
□ Pickup time window
□ What happens on stock-out or quality rejection
□ Trial period length (recommended 15–30 days)

E. RED FLAGS – WALK AWAY IF:
• Supplier refuses any written confirmation
• Prices keep changing daily without notice
• Quality is inconsistent in the first week
• They demand advance payment or large deposit
• They cannot confirm orders within 15–20 minutes

F. POST-NEGOTIATION ACTIONS
1. Send WhatsApp summary of agreed terms the same day
2. Add partner to “JG Mart Partners” group
3. Run 3–5 test orders before full commitment
4. Review performance after 15 days and decide to continue or exit

PART 4: WEEKLY SETTLEMENT PROCESS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Every Sunday:
1. JG Mart sends weekly order summary via WhatsApp
2. Partner verifies all orders and amounts
3. Any discrepancies resolved within 24 hours
4. Payment made by Tuesday via bKash or cash
5. Payment = Total wholesale value – Commission

Example:
Weekly wholesale value: 50,000 BDT
Average commission 12%: 6,000 BDT
Partner receives: 44,000 BDT
