Use this exact sequence on the first real soft-launch day.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
DAY BEFORE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

□ All physical print pack ready
□ 3+ partners confirmed for tomorrow
□ Opening float counted and ready
□ WhatsApp announcement sent to first 30–50 people
□ Brother briefed and has his clipboard

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
LAUNCH DAY – MORNING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

07:30 – 08:00
• Final check of float, bags, phone charge
• Confirm partner availability via WhatsApp

08:00 – 09:00
• Receive first orders
• Build consolidated pickup list
• Decide partner sequence

09:00 – 09:15
• Give float + list + checklist to Brother
• Stand-up: confirm plan and timing

09:15 – 11:00
• Brother executes pickup using Checklist
• You stay available on WhatsApp for any quality decisions

11:00 – 11:30
• Brother returns
• Joint quality check using Quality Standard Card
• Sort by building using Delivery Sheet
• Reconcile cash so far

11:30 – 13:00
• Delivery run
• Collect COD
• Note any access or customer issues

13:00 – 13:30
• Return, final cash count
• Fill Daily Ops Dashboard (morning numbers)
• Short review: what went well / what to fix for evening

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
LAUNCH DAY – EVENING (if running second shift)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

15:00 – 15:30
• Collect evening orders
• Confirm partners again

15:30 – 17:30
• Pickup cycle (same discipline)

17:30 – 19:30
• Sort + Deliver

19:30 – 20:00
• Full cash reconciliation
• Complete Daily Ops Dashboard
• Write top 3 lessons from Day 1
• Decide tomorrow’s one improvement

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
END OF DAY 1 SUCCESS CRITERIA
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

• At least 5–8 successful deliveries
• Zero major quality complaints
• Cash fully reconciled
• Brother completed the loop with checklist
• Dashboard filled

If these are met → continue Day 2 with small volume increase.
If not → pause, fix the broken step, then resume.
