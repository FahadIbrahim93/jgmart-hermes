SECTION 3: PAYMENT DISPUTE RESOLUTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CUSTOMER SAYS "I ALREADY PAID" BUT YOU DON'T HAVE THE MONEY:

  1. NEVER accuse the customer of lying
  2. Say: "Let me check — sometimes bKash has a delay. Can you share the
     transaction ID from your bKash app? I'll verify with bKash and get
     back to you within 30 minutes."
  3. Check your bKash app for the transaction
  4. If found: apologize for the confusion, all good
  5. If not found:
     a. Ask customer to check their "Sent Money" history
     b. If they show a screenshot with transaction ID, forward to bKash support (16247)
     c. If they can't prove it: offer 50/50 split — "Let's split the difference.
        Pay half now, I'll cover the rest."

CUSTOMER HAS WRONG CHANGE FOR COD:

  1. Always carry 500 BDT in small notes (20s and 50s)
  2. If customer can't make exact change:
     a. Accept what they have and note the difference
     b. Message them later: "You still owe X BDT. You can send via bKash or
        pay next time."
  3. Never argue over small amounts (< 50 BDT) — absorb the loss

SECTION 4: MONSOON / HEAVY RAIN PROTOCOL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BEFORE RAINY SEASON:
  □ Buy 5 large waterproof delivery bags
  □ Buy rider rain poncho + boot covers
  □ Identify flooded routes on your delivery map
  □ Keep spare dry clothes at the hub

DURING RAIN:
  1. Protect ALL orders with plastic wrap before leaving market
  2. Message customers: "Heavy rain — deliveries may be 20-30 min late."
  3. Never ride through water deeper than your bike's exhaust
  4. If road is flooded: park and walk the last 100m
  5. If customer refuses to come down in rain: leave at guard/reception,
     take a photo, send to customer

AFTER RAIN:
  1. Check every delivery bag for water damage
  2. If any items damaged: replace or refund (absorb the cost)
  3. Dry all bags before next use
  4. Log rain-related delays in your tracker
