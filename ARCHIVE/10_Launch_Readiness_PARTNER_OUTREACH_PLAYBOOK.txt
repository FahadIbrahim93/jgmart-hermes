1. UNDERSTANDING THE SHOP OWNER MINDSET
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Before you pitch, know what's in their head:

  Pain Points:
  • Limited to foot traffic — they only sell to whoever walks past their shop
  • Weather kills sales — rain = empty streets = no customers
  • Younger generation doesn't visit markets — their kids order online
  • Cash flow fluctuations — busy some days, dead others
  • Storage/waste — unsold perishables are lost money
  • Competition from other shops in the same market

  What They Want:
  • More customers without working harder
  • Extra income with minimal effort
  • Quick, reliable payment
  • Respect and professional treatment
  • Someone who solves problems, not creates them

  What They Fear:
  • Getting scammed or not paid
  • Complicated systems/technology
  • Having to change how they operate
  • "Platforms" taking over (Amazon/Flipkart anxiety)
  • Loss of control over their customers

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. SHOP TYPES & PRIORITY RANKING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Priority 1 (Recruit First — High Volume, High Frequency):
  ⭐ Vegetable vendors         — Daily demand, impulse purchases, high margins
  ⭐ Butchers                   — Regular buyers, premium products
  ⭐ Fish/Seafood vendors       — Premium pricing, recurring
  ⭐ Rice/Grain wholesalers     — Bulk orders, stable revenue

Priority 2 (Recruit Next — Medium Volume):
  ⭐ Dairy shops                — Milk, yogurt, cheese — recurring orders
  ⭐ Spice/grocery shops        — Pantry staples, long shelf life
  ⭐ Bakery                     — Morning orders, impulse add-on

Priority 3 (Nice to Have — Niche):
  ⭐ Fruit vendors              — Seasonal, but high-margin add-ons
  ⭐ Snack/farsan shops         — Good for upselling
  ⭐ Tea/coffee suppliers       — Bundle opportunity

3. APPROACH STRATEGY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Timing:
  • Best time to visit: 10 AM - 12 PM or 3 PM - 5 PM (not peak rush hours)
  • Avoid: Early morning (7-9 AM), lunch (1-2 PM), closing time (7-8 PM)
  • Day of week: Tuesday-Thursday are best. Friday = half-day. Sunday = closed
    or very slow for some.

Who Should Go:
  • Fahad or brother ONLY — don't send a rider to close a deal
  • Wear clean, presentable clothes (not formal suit, but neat)
  • Bring a notebook, pen, and a printed one-pager about JG Mart

Setting the Stage:
  • First visit: introduce yourself, explain what JG Mart does in ONE sentence.
    Don't overwhelm. Build rapport first.
  • Second visit (if needed): bring the pitch with numbers
  • Close on third visit at latest — move fast

4. THE PITCH — WHAT TO SAY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Opening (30 seconds):
  "আমি ফাহাদ, JG Mart এর প্রতিষ্ঠাতা। আমরা জাপান গার্ডেন সিটির residents-কে
   ঘর বসে সবকিছু দিয়ে দিচ্ছি — directly কৃষি মার্কেট থেকে। আপনারা আমাদের
   delivery partner হবেন। আপনারা আর বেশি sell করতে পারবেন, নিজেরা ছাড়া
   কোনো extra কাজ করতে হবে না।"

  English translation:
  "I'm Fahad, founder of JG Mart. We deliver everything to Japan Garden City
   residents directly from Krishi Market. You'll be our delivery partner. You'll
   sell more without any extra work."

The Value Proposition (60 seconds):
  "What we do: We take orders on WhatsApp from JGC residents → we buy from you
   at YOUR price → we deliver to their door → we pay you the same day or next day.

  What you get:
  • Extra customers — people who don't come to the market
  • No effort — you just prepare their order, we pick it up
  • Instant/same-day payment
  • You keep your existing customers — we add to them, not replace
  • No fees to join, no monthly charges"

How You Make Money for Them (translate to their language):
  "আপনার ежедневের customers + আমাদের নতুন online customers =每月আপনার sales বাড়বে।
   আপনার দাম আপনার। আমরা শুধু take করি delivery fee এবং customer-কে আমরা
   extra charge করি delivery charge।"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5. COMMISSION & PRICE NEGOTIATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Commission Structure (What JG Mart charges the customer — not the shop):
  • JG Mart markup: 10-20% over shop price (or fixed delivery fee + markup)
  • JG Mart pays partner: THEIR listed price (no commission from them!)
  • THIS IS CRITICAL — never say "we take commission from you"
  • Instead: "You keep your price. We make money on delivery fees and markup
    charged to customers."

  Why this works: Shop owners HATE giving away margin. Frame it as:
  "We add new customers for you. We charge THEM a delivery fee. You get your
  regular price. No loss for you."

Negotiation Tactics:

  Tactic 1 — The Pilot Offer:
  "Let's try for 2 weeks. No commitment. If you don't like it, we part ways.
   If it works, we continue. No risk for you."

  Tactic 2 — The Social Proof:
  "Shop X and Shop Y in the same market are already working with us. They're
   getting 5-10 extra orders per day."

  Tactic 3 — The Easy Start:
  "We start with just 2-3 items from your shop. Whatever you want. We don't
   need your full catalog. You pick what works."

  Tactic 4 — The Immediate Payment:
  "We pay you immediately after delivery — same day cash or bKash. No waiting
   30 days like wholesale buyers."

  Tactic 5 — Low Risk:
  "If an order is wrong or customer complains, we handle it. You don't deal
   with angry customers. We take the responsibility."

6. HANDLING OBJECTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Objection: "Online শপিং এ আমার customer কমবে।" (My customers will decrease)
Response: "No — this is ADDITIONAL customers. People who don't come to the market
  at all. We're reaching a different audience. Your walk-in customers stay."

Objection: "Payment কখন পাব?" (When do I get paid?)
Response: "Same day or next day. Cash or bKash. We don't hold your money for
  15-30 days like big wholesalers. We pick up your order and pay immediately."

Objection: "Technical confusion — আমি technology বুঝি না।" (I don't understand tech)
Response: "You don't need to do anything on phone. We WhatsApp YOU the order.
  You prepare it. We come pick it up. That's it. Simple."

Objection: "What if customer doesn't pay or complains?"
Response: "We handle everything. If there's an issue, we deal with the customer.
  You get paid regardless. We take the risk."

Objection: "আমার শপ ছোট, আমার দাম বাড়াবো?" (My shop is small, should I raise prices?)
Response: "No — your price stays the same. We set the delivery charge separately.
  Customers pay for delivery, not for your product. You don't change anything."

Objection: "What about returns or wrong items?"
Response: "We have a simple rule: if it's our mistake (wrong item), we cover it.
  If it's your shop's mistake (wrong weight/quality), you cover it. We'll agree
  on a fair process. Usually a 10-15% buffer for errors is enough."

Objection: "কেন আপনার পlene দিয়ে যাব?" (Why should I trust you?)
Response: "I live in this area. My family is here. This is my business. I need
  you more than you need me — without shops, I have no business. We're in this
  together. Here's my number, call me anytime."

7. TRIAL PERIOD STRUCTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Recommended: 2-week trial with no long-term commitment.

Week 1 — Soft Start:
  • 2-3 items from their shop in your catalog
  • You handle ALL pickup (Fahad/brother personally go to shop)
  • Orders go to top 3-4 shops only
  • Goal: Build confidence, test process, iron out issues
  • Track: order count per shop, issues, fulfillment time

Week 2 — Expansion:
  • Add 2-3 more items from each shop
  • Start introducing riders for pickup
  • Goal: Get to 3-5 orders per shop per day
  • Track: same metrics + customer satisfaction

Trial End — Decision Day:
  If both sides are happy → Sign 3-month agreement
  If not working → Honest conversation, part ways cleanly

Trial Success Metrics (per shop):
  ☑ Minimum 5 orders in 2-week period
  ☑ Less than 3 issues (wrong item, quality complaint)
  ☑ Shop owner responds within 30 minutes to order messages
  ☑ Shop can prepare orders within 15 minutes
  ☑ Shop owner is pleasant to work with

9. RED FLAGS — WALK AWAY FROM THESE SHOPS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🚩 They demand commission from YOU (not just markup on customer side)
🚩 They want to set prices for you or control customer pricing
🚩 They refuse to give you their price list or give inconsistent pricing
🚩 They consistently deliver wrong/defective products
🚩 They take 30+ minutes to prepare an order
🚩 They don't respond to WhatsApp/phone for 2+ hours
🚩 They want exclusive rights (don't let any single shop lock you in)
🚩 They demand advance payment or deposits
🚩 They have a bad reputation with other shop owners
🚩 They ask for "under the table" arrangements or tax evasion help

10. ONBOARDING A NEW PARTNER — STEP BY STEP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Step 1 — First Contact (Day 1):
  Visit in person. Introduce JG Mart. Listen to their business. Don't pitch yet.

Step 2 — Pitch (Day 2-3):
  Return with one-pager. Explain the model. Answer questions.

Step 3 — Agreement (Day 4-5):
  Simple 1-page agreement. Key terms: pricing, payment timeline, how orders come,
  how disputes are handled. Both sign.

Step 4 — Setup (Day 5-6):
  Get their price list (WhatsApp or photo). Input into JG Mart catalog. Take
  product photos. Add to WhatsApp catalog.

Step 5 — Training (Day 6-7):
  Show them how orders will come: "You'll get a WhatsApp message like this..."
  Show them what a typical order looks like. Explain packing standards.

Step 6 — Soft Launch (Day 7-10):
  Start sending test orders (internal team orders first). Make sure process works.

Step 7 — Live (Day 11+):
  Start taking real customer orders for this shop. Monitor daily.

11. PARTNER AGREEMENT — KEY CLAUSES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Every agreement should include:
  1. Parties: JG Mart [name] and [Shop Name]
  2. Term: 3 months, auto-renewing unless 7-day notice
  3. Pricing: Shop's regular retail price (JG Mart pays as listed)
  4. Order Process: Orders come via WhatsApp to [shop number]. Shop confirms
     within 15 minutes.
  5. Payment: Within 24 hours of delivery confirmation (bKash/cash)
  6. Quality: Shop guarantees fresh, standard-quality products
  7. Returns: Customer complaints resolved within 2 hours. Shared responsibility
     model (see dispute resolution)
  8. Exclusivity: None — shop can sell to anyone. JG Mart can partner with anyone.
  9. Termination: 7-day written notice by either party
  10. Dispute Resolution: First try to resolve mutually. Escalate to Fahad.

13. THE PARTNER MINDSET — THINK LONG-TERM

Remember:
  • These are YOUR business partners, not just vendors
  • Treat them with respect. Visit their shops. Learn their names.
  • Pay on time, always. Even if JG Mart is struggling.
  • When they have a problem, help solve it (festival stock, family issues, etc.)
  • Celebrate their wins ("Your biryani masala is our #1 seller this week!")
  • Give feedback privately, praise publicly
  • A loyal partner who's been with you for 2 years is worth more than 10
    new partners who quit after 2 weeks
