1. JOB DESCRIPTION & EXPECTATIONS
================================================================================

ROLE: Delivery Rider (JG Mart)
You are the face of JG Mart. Every customer interaction shapes our brand.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES
  • Pick up orders from partners (P001–P005) on time
  • Deliver orders to 4 clusters within promised ETA
  • Collect cash (COD) or confirm bKash payments
  • Handle customer queries politely and professionally
  • Maintain bike, delivery bag, and uniform in good condition
  • Report issues to Ops Lead immediately (stock problems, wrong items, delays)
  • Follow all safety rules (helmet, traffic laws, no phone while riding)

WORKING HOURS
  • Shift 1: 06:00 – 13:00
  • Shift 2: 13:00 – 20:00
  • Overtime only with Founder approval (BDT 50/hour extra)
  • Lunch break: 30 mins (mid-shift, coordinated with Ops Lead)

EXPECTED STANDARDS
  - Attendance: < 1 unplanned absence per month
  - Punctuality: Arrive on time; notify Ops Lead if running late
  - Appearance: Clean JG Mart uniform, helmet, trimmed nails
  - Phone: Always charged, WhatsApp active, tracker app running
  - Integrity: Zero tolerance for theft, cash mishandling, or order fraud

3. CUSTOMER INTERACTION GUIDELINES
================================================================================

GREETING (In person or WhatsApp)
  "Hi [Name]! I'm [Your Name] from JG Mart. Here's your order. Thank you!"

DELIVERY PROCESS
  1. Smile, greet, confirm name
  2. Hand over items + bill/receipt
  3. If COD: "Your total is BDT [Amount]. Thank you!"
  4. If bKash: "Please confirm payment. Thank you!"
  5. Wait for customer to close door before leaving
  6. Mark order "Delivered" in tracker immediately

HANDLING CUSTOMER QUESTIONS
  - "Where is my order?" → "I'm on my way, ETA [Time]. I'll send you a message when I'm 5 minutes away."
  - "Can I add items?" → "I'm sorry, I can't modify orders after dispatch. You can place a new order for next delivery."
  - "Why is it late?" → "Apologies for the delay. We had [brief reason]. Your new ETA is [Time]. Thank you for your patience."
  - "The item is damaged" → "I'm sorry! Please send a photo to our WhatsApp. We'll arrange a replacement or refund immediately."

WHAT TO DO IF CUSTOMER IS ANGRY
  1. Stay calm, do not argue
  2. Listen fully without interrupting
  3. Apologize sincerely: "I'm very sorry for this experience."
  4. Say: "Let me call my manager and fix this for you right now."
  5. Call Ops Lead immediately (do not promise anything beyond your authority)
  6. If customer records video, do not obstruct; cooperate fully

4. CASH HANDLING PROCEDURES
================================================================================

COD COLLECTION
  - State amount clearly: "Your total is BDT [Amount]."
  - Count change back to customer in front of them
  - Hand receipt immediately: "Here's your receipt. Thank you!"
  - Never put cash in delivery bag with items; keep in secure inner pocket

CASH STORAGE DURING SHIFT
  - Use hidden zippered pouch inside jacket/waist bag
  - Do NOT keep cash in bike basket, side pocket, or visible bag
  - If cash exceeds BDT 15,000, inform Ops Lead for top-up collection

END-OF-DAY CASH COUNT
  1. Return to hub, sit with Ops Lead
  2. Empty pouch completely onto clean table
  3. Count by denomination together (BDT 1000 → 500 → 200 → 100 → 50 → 20 → 10)
  4. Ops Lead writes total in Delivery Log; you sign
  5. Cash sealed in envelope, signed by both
  6. Any shortage/explained immediately and honestly

IF CUSTOMER CLAIMS THEY PAID BUT YOU DON'T HAVE CASH
  1. Do not argue; say: "Let me check my records and call you back."
  2. Return to hub, check tracker + delivery log
  3. If tracker shows "unpaid": Call Ops Lead, investigate together
  4. If tracker shows "paid" but cash missing: Report immediately (theft suspicion)

5. BIKE MAINTENANCE CHECKLIST
================================================================================

PRE-SHIFT DAILY CHECK (5 mins)
  □ Tires: Press both tires; if soft, inflate or report puncture
  □ Brakes: Squeeze levers; if spongy, report to Ops Lead
  □ Chain: Look for rust or loose links; oil if dry (carry mini oil bottle)
  □ Lights: Test headlight + brake light at night
  □ Fuel / Charge: Check meter; refill/recharge if < 50%
  □ Mirrors: Adjust to see traffic behind you
  □ Horn: Test; if broken, report immediately
  □ Stand: Ensure bike can stand on center stand

WEEKLY CHECK (Weekends)
  □ Wash bike thoroughly (especially chain area)
  □ Check tire pressure with gauge (32–35 PSI normal)
  □ Inspect brake pads for wear
  □ Test all electronics (indicator, speedometer, phone mount)
  □ Clean helmet visor + delivery bag

MONTHLY SERVICE
  □ Take bike to trusted mechanic for full service
  □ Oil change, chain replacement check, brake fluid top-up
  □ Get service receipt; submit to Ops Lead for reimbursement

WHAT TO DO IF BIKE BREAKS DOWN
  1. Move bike to safe side of road immediately
  2. Call Ops Lead: "Bike broken at [location]. Orders affected: [list]."
  3. Call customer for each affected order: "Apologies, bike issue. New ETA [Time] or redelivery tomorrow."
  4. Ops Lead arranges backup bike or transfers orders
  5. Do NOT attempt risky repairs on busy road; wait for help

6. WHAT TO DO IF...
================================================================================

CUSTOMER NOT HOME
  1. Call customer (2 rings max, no spam)
  2. If no answer, WhatsApp: "Hi [Name], I'm at your door with your order. Please come down or let me know where to leave it. 📦"
  3. Take photo of items at door + house number visible
  4. If no response in 15 mins, return to hub
  5. Mark order "Redeliver – Customer Not Home" in tracker
  6. Redeliver next day same time (no extra charge) OR customer picks up from hub

WRONG ADDRESS / CUSTOMER SAYS "I DIDN'T ORDER"
  1. Stay calm; do not leave items unattended
  2. Show order details on your phone: name, items, address
  3. Call Ops Lead: "Customer at [wrong address] says they didn't order. Order ID: [XXX]."
  4. Ops Lead calls customer to confirm correct address
  5. If genuine mistake: Deliver to correct address immediately
  6. If fraud: Return items to hub, Ops Lead handles refund

PAYMENT DISPUTE (Customer says they paid bKash but you see no TrxID)
  1. Do not take their word; check tracker + bKash statement
  2. Say: "Let me verify with our team. Please show me your bKash confirmation if you have it."
  3. If customer shows screenshot with different number/amount: Report to Ops Lead
  4. If no screenshot: Say "Please send it to our WhatsApp within 10 mins or I'll have to return this order."
  5. Never leave order without payment resolution

VEHICLE BREAKDOWN
  1. Move to safe spot; turn on hazard lights
  2. Call Ops Lead immediately with location + affected orders
  3. If minor issue (puncture, chain): Fix if safe, else push bike to nearest shop
  4. If major issue (engine, electrical): Wait for backup rider
  5. Ops Lead notifies customers of delay; offers reschedule or discount

RAIN / SEVERE WEATHER
  1. Slow down, use headlight, avoid flooded areas
  2. Cover order with rain bag before leaving bike
  3. If rain too heavy: Call Ops Lead for weather hold decision
  4. Never ride through water deeper than ankle; risk engine damage
  5. If phone gets wet: Seal in plastic bag immediately, dry at hub

7. DOS AND DON'TS LIST
================================================================================

✅ DOS
  • Wear helmet and JG Mart uniform every shift
  • Greet customers with a smile
  • Double-check address before leaving hub
  • Keep phone on silent while riding, use Bluetooth if available
  • Report issues to Ops Lead within 5 mins
  • Count cash honestly in presence of Ops Lead
  • Thank every customer, even if they complain
  • Take photos of proof-of-delivery (WhatsApp send or file)
  • Keep delivery bag clean and organized
  • Ask for help when you don't know something

❌ DON'TS
  • Never ride without helmet or uniform
  • Never argue with customers, even if they are wrong
  • Never accept tips or personal favors from customers
  • Never leave bike unlocked or items unattended
  • Never use personal phone for customer orders (privacy + billing)
  • Never eat/drink while riding
  • Never overspeed or run red lights (safety first)
  • Never share customer phone numbers with anyone
  • Never open cash envelope without Ops Lead present
  • Never say "I don't know" – say "Let me check with my team and call you back"
