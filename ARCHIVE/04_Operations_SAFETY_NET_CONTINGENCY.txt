SCENARIO 1: BROTHER GETS SICK (RIDER UNAVAILABLE)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

IMMEDIATE (within 15 minutes):
  [ ] Call backup rider [NAME] — [PHONE]
  [ ] If available: brief backup on route + deliveries
  [ ] If not available: Call second backup [NAME] — [PHONE]
  [ ] If no backup available: YOU become the rider today

WITH 30 MINUTES:
  [ ] Message all morning customers:
      "Fahad here! My brother is unwell today. I'll be delivering
      personally. Please expect a slight delay of 20-30 minutes.
      Sorry for the inconvenience! 🙏"
  [ ] Adjust delivery schedule (add 30 min buffer)
  [ ] Call partners: delay pickup by 30 minutes

PREVENTION:
  → Hire backup rider as soon as orders hit 15+/day
  → Train backup rider on routes during slow days
  → Have 2 backup contacts always ready

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SCENARIO 2: KRISHI MARKET PARTNER DROPS OUT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

IMMEDIATE (within 30 minutes):
  [ ] Identify alternative supplier in Krishi Market for that category
  [ ] Visit or call alternative: "Bhai, I have urgent orders for [items]"
  [ ] Accept first available supplier at market price

WITHIN 2 HOURS:
  [ ] Message affected customers:
      "[Partner name] had an issue today. I've sourced from [new partner]
      at Krishi Market directly. Quality is verified. Thanks for your
      patience! 🙏"
  [ ] Update partner database: flag primary as "unavailable"
  [ ] Begin search for new primary partner within 3 days

PREVENTION:
  → Always have 2 approved backup vendors per category
  → Build relationships with 2+ partners in each category
  → Visit 1 new potential partner every week

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SCENARIO 3: CUSTOMER COMPLAINTS (QUALITY ISSUE)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

IMMEDIATE (within 5 minutes of complaint):
  [ ] Apologise immediately (no excuses, no blame-shifting)
  [ ] Ask for photo of the issue (via WhatsApp)
  [ ] Decide resolution:

  MINOR (wrong brand, slightly overripe):
    → Apologise + offer 30 BDT credit on next order
    → Fix for next delivery

  MODERATE (spoiled produce, wrong item):
    → Refund that item's cost
    → Offer 50 BDT credit on next order
    → Replace at next delivery slot

  CRITICAL (food safety concern, major quality failure):
    → Full order refund
    → Extra delivery on the house
    → Personal call from Fahad

WITHIN 1 HOUR:
  [ ] Resolve with customer
  [ ] Log in COMPLAINT_LOG: date, customer, issue, resolution, cost
  [ ] Call partner — share photo, discuss quality expectations
  [ ] If repeated issue with same partner: suspend for 2 days

PREVENTION:
  → 3-stage quality check: at pickup, at sorting, at delivery
  → Check produce at pickup in front of partner
  → Photo of packed order before leaving market

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SCENARIO 4: bKASH / PAYMENT DISPUTE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

IMMEDIATE:
  [ ] Check bKash transaction history
  [ ] If payment not received: politely ask customer to check
  [ ] If customer insists they paid: delay resolution to evening
  [ ] In evening: sit with customer, check their bKash together

ESCALATION:
  [ ] For COD disputes: customer signature on receipt is final
  [ ] For bKash: transaction ID is proof
  [ ] If customer refuses to pay: warn → offer split → last resort: forgive

PREVENTION:
  → Always send bKash request number to customer
  → Take signature on receipt for ALL COD deliveries
  → Never hand over goods without payment

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SCENARIO 5: LOW ORDER VOLUME (UNDER 5 ORDERS IN A SLOT)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

IMMEDIATE:
  [ ] Don't cancel the slot — deliver what you have
  [ ] Every low-volume day is a marketing opportunity
  [ ] Use the extra time to:
      → Visit 2 new potential partners
      → Hand out flyers in Buildings 4-8
      → Call 5 existing customers: "How was your last order?"
      → Post in building WhatsApp groups

NEXT DAY:
  [ ] Run a promotion: "Free delivery this week only!"
  [ ] Ask 3 loyal customers for referrals personally
  [ ] Go door-to-door in 2 buildings (15 min each)

PREVENTION:
  → Daily WhatsApp status: "Still time for evening slot!"
  → Referral programme is your growth engine
  → Week 1 target: 5 orders/slot minimum

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SCENARIO 6: CASH / THEFT / LOSS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

IMMEDIATE:
  [ ] Confirm amount lost
  [ ] Retrace steps — check delivery bags, pockets, vehicle
  [ ] Check CCTV if at building entrance
  [ ] Check with rider (if not you)

RESOLUTION:
  [ ] Amount < 500 BDT: absorb as cost of business
  [ ] Amount 500-2,000 BDT: split loss between you and rider (50/50)
  [ ] Amount 2,000+ BDT: investigate fully, involve building committee if needed

PREVENTION:
  → Cash counted and logged at 3 points: pickup, before delivery, after delivery
  → Daily cash cap per rider: 5,000 BDT
  → Transfer excess to bKash during lunch break
  → Receipt signed for EVERY order

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SCENARIO 7: MONSOON / HEAVY RAIN
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

IMMEDIATE (if rain hits during pickup or delivery):
  [ ] Protect all orders: cover bags with plastic sheet
  [ ] Message customers: "Heavy rain — deliveries may be delayed 20-30 min"
  [ ] Avoid flooded routes (check building WhatsApp groups for updates)
  [ ] Delay or cancel if water level unsafe for bike

COMPENSATION:
  [ ] 10 BDT discount for every 30 min late due to rain
  [ ] OR offer free delivery on next order

PREVENTION:
  → Monsoon kit: rider carries rain poncho + large plastic bags
  → Protect bag with waterproof cover
  → Check weather at 6AM and 1PM daily
  → Have cash ready for alternative transport (CNG) if needed

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SCENARIO 8: WHATSAPP BAN / NUMBER BLOCKED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

IMMEDIATE:
  [ ] Switch to backup phone number immediately
  [ ] Inform all customers via SMS (if you have their number) or building group
  [ ] Post updated number on Facebook page + building groups
  [ ] Begin WhatsApp support ticket to restore original number

RECOVERY:
  [ ] Original number may be restored in 1-6 hours (spam ban)
  [ ] If permanently banned: migrate to new number + update everywhere
  [ ] Update business cards, flyers, all assets

PREVENTION:
  → Never spam — only reply to individual queries
  → Don't send bulk messages to 50+ people at once
  → Use broadcast lists (max 256) instead of groups
  → Have a backup SIM ready at all times
  → Register for WhatsApp Business API (free) as long-term solution

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SCENARIO 9: COMPETITOR ENTERS JGC
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SIGNS TO WATCH:
  [ ] Ads in JGC mentioning "free delivery" or "grocery"
  [ ] Flyers on building notice boards
  [ ] Neighbours mentioning a new service
  [ ] Call volume drops while building population hasn't changed

RESPONSE:
  [ ] DO NOT panic — you have an 800m advantage
  [ ] DO NOT start a price war — you'll both bleed
  [ ] DO double down on community: mentions in groups, personal service
  [ ] Offer a loyalty program: every 10th order free
  [ ] Faster response times (you're a resident, they're not)
  [ ] Partner exclusivity: ask partners not to work with both

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SCENARIO 10: CASH RUNS OUT BEFORE BREAKEVEN
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RED FLAGS (watch for these):
  [ ] Cash dropped below 100,000 BDT
  [ ] Month 3 projection shows loss instead of profit
  [ ] Fixed costs above 70K/month
  [ ] Orders per day below 15 consistently

ACTIONS:
  [ ] Cut all non-essential spending immediately
  [ ] Pause marketing (you have community word-of-mouth)
  [ ] Reduce manager hours
  [ ] Offer prepaid subscription packages (collect cash upfront)
  [ ] Launch a mini-fundraise from building committee members
  [ ] If desperate: Fahad takes a part-time job, brother runs ops
