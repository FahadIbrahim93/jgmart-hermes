## QUICK REFERENCE

- [ ] Subject line includes "Seed Investment" + "৳250K"
- [ ] Opening hooks with problem (not solution)
- [ ] 3-4 bullet points max
- [ ] Clear ask (meeting / next step)
- [ ] Contact info in signature

- [ ] Clear value prop for them (not just you)
- [ ] Low-friction ask (2-week trial)

- [ ] Your phone/WhatsApp prominent

## BEST PRACTICES

1. **Keep it short** — Investors get 100 emails/day. 150 words max.
2. **Lead with the problem** — Not your solution. Hook them with the pain point.
3. **Be specific** — "1,700 families" not "many families"
4. **Clear ask** — Tell them exactly what you want (meeting, reply, decision)
5. **Follow up in 3 days** — If no reply, send a brief follow-up
6. **Use WhatsApp for speed** — Email for formal, WhatsApp for quick replies
7. **Personalize** — Reference their company, their work, your connection
8. **No attachments first** — Send the email, then offer deck if interested
