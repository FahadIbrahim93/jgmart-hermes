## BROADCAST STRATEGY

### Frequency Guide
| Template | Frequency | Best Time |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| Launch Announcement | Once | Day 1, 8:00 AM |
| Building Launch | Once per building | Staggered Day 1-3 |
| Morning Specials | Daily | 7:00 AM |
| Evening Specials | Daily | 2:00 PM |
| Referral Launch | Once | Week 2 |
| Referral Reminder | Weekly | Friday, 4:00 PM |
| Weekly Reminder | Weekly | Friday, 7:00 AM |
| Re-engagement | Weekly | Monday, 10:00 AM |
| Bulk Discount | Weekly | Wednesday, 11:00 AM |
| New Products | As needed | When added |
| Service Pause | As needed | Immediately |
| Service Resumed | As needed | When resumed |

### Best Practices
1. **Don't spam** — Max 1-2 broadcasts per day
2. **Segment** — Send targeted messages to specific buildings
3. **Personalize** — Use customer names when possible
4. **Test timing** — Find when your audience is most active
5. **Track responses** — Note which templates get most replies
6. **Use WhatsApp Business** — For bulk sending, use WhatsApp Business API
7. **Avoid all-caps** — It looks like shouting
8. **Keep it short** — Under 200 words for best engagement

### Response Rate Optimization
- Add question: "Reply YES if you're interested!"
- Use emojis sparingly (2-3 per message)
- Include clear CTA (call-to-action)
- Offer incentive (discount, free delivery)
- Make it easy to respond (simple yes/no)
