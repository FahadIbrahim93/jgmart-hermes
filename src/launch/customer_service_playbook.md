# JG Mart — Customer Service Playbook
## Tone: Friendly, Fast, Professional | Language: Bengali + English

---

## CORE PRINCIPLES

1. **Respond within 30 minutes** — even if just to say "Got your order, confirming now"
2. **Be human, not a bot** — use emojis, be warm, sign with your name
3. **Solve first, explain later** — fix the problem, then explain what happened
4. **Over-deliver** — if something goes wrong, make it right with a bonus
5. **Remember regulars** — greet returning customers by name

## COMMON SCENARIOS

### 1. Order Not Received in Time
**Customer:** "Where's my order? It's 1PM already!"
**Response:**
```
Sorry for the delay! 😔

Your order #[ID] is on its way. Our rider is [X] minutes away.
We had [brief reason: heavy traffic / high order volume].

As a sorry, here's ৳20 off your next order. 🙏

ETA: [time]
```

### 2. Wrong Item Delivered
**Customer:** "You gave me the wrong chicken!"
**Response:**
```
Oh no, I'm so sorry! 🙏

We'll bring the correct [item] within [time].
We'll also give you [item] free for the trouble.

Or if you prefer, we'll refund ৳[amount] to your bKash.

Which works better for you?
```

### 3. Missing Item
**Customer:** "You forgot my onions!"
**Response:**
```
So sorry! 🙏

We missed [item] in your order #[ID].
We'll add it to your next delivery for free.

Or if you need it today, we can send Rider 2 within 30 minutes.

What works for you?
```

### 4. Quality Issue
**Customer:** "The tomatoes are all mushy 😞"
**Response:**
```
I'm really sorry about that! 🙏

We stand by our quality — we'll refund ৳[amount] for the tomatoes right away.
No need to return them.

We're also noting this with our Krishi Market vendor to improve quality.

Is there anything else we can do for you today?
```

### 5. Price Dispute
**Customer:** "You charged ৳150 for onions but it was ৳120 last week!"
**Response:**
```
Thanks for catching that! 🙏

You're right — we updated prices yesterday due to [reason: market rates / seasonal changes].
But we should have notified you.

We'll refund the ৳30 difference to your bKash.
And for today, we'll give you 10% off your total order.

Sorry again!
```

### 6. Late Delivery
**Customer:** "You're 2 hours late!"
**Response:**
```
I'm truly sorry for the delay. 😔

We had [brief reason: unexpected traffic / high order volume / rider issue].
Your order is now [on its way / arriving in X minutes].

We're crediting ৳20 to your account for the inconvenience.

This won't happen again — we're [adding a rider / optimizing routes] to prevent this.

Thank you for your patience. 🙏
```

### 7. Cancellation Request
**Customer:** "I need to cancel my order"
**Response:**
```
No problem! Let me check your order #[ID].

Since it's before [9AM / 3:30PM], we can cancel with full refund.
If it's after cutoff, there's a 50% fee.

When do you need to cancel by?
[Process refund if applicable]

Hope to serve you again soon! 🙏
```

### 8. Referral Inquiry
**Customer:** "How does the referral program work?"
**Response:**
```
Great question! 🎉

Share your unique code: [CODE]
Friend uses it on their first order:
- They get ৳50 off
- You get ৳50 off your next order

Share via WhatsApp: [wa.me link]
Copy code: [CODE]

Unlimited referrals — share with all your neighbors! 🏠
```

## DO'S AND DON'TS

### ✅ DO
- Respond quickly, even if just to acknowledge
- Use customer's name
- Be empathetic and apologetic when things go wrong
- Offer compensation proactively
- Follow up after resolution
- Thank them for their patience

### ❌ DON'T
- Make excuses without taking responsibility
- Blame the rider/vendor publicly
- Argue about prices or policies
- Ignore messages (even if busy, send "BRB")
- Promise what you can't deliver
- Use all-caps or aggressive language

## CONTACT ESCALATION

If you can't resolve an issue:
1. Take customer's phone number
2. Say: "Let me escalate this to our founder, Fahad. He'll call you within 30 minutes."
3. Send WhatsApp to Fahad with:
   - Customer name + phone
   - Issue summary
   - What you've already tried
4. Follow up with customer: "Fahad will call you shortly"

Never leave a customer hanging.
