## EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS

### Rider No-Show
1. Call backup rider immediately
2. Merge routes if possible
3. Notify affected customers
4. Offer 20 BDT discount

### Vendor Shortage
1. Substitute equivalent product
2. Notify customer before delivery
3. Refund difference if cheaper

### Website Down
1. Manual WhatsApp orders
2. Phone notes for tracking
3. Fix within 2 hours

### Payment Issue
1. Accept cash only
2. Process bKash manually later
3. Update customer
