## Slide 1: Title
**Time:** 15 seconds
**Say:** "Good morning. I'm Fahad, founder of JG Mart. Today I'm going to show you how we're bringing wholesale groceries to 1,700 families in Japan Garden City — at 30-50% cheaper than retail. We're raising 250K BDT seed capital for 15% equity."

**Don't say:** Don't lead with the money. Lead with the problem.

## Delivery Tips

1. **Start with the problem, not the solution.** Investors see 100 "Uber for X" pitches. Yours is different because you start with a real, visceral problem.
2. **Show, don't tell.** Open the catalog on your phone. Let them see the product.
3. **Be authentic.** You're not a Silicon Valley founder. You're a JGC resident solving a JGC problem. That's your strength.
4. **Know your numbers.** 51% margin, ৳800 AOV, 250K ask — memorize these cold.
5. **Handle objections gracefully.** Use the Q&A cheat sheet. Don't get defensive.
6. **End with a clear ask.** Don't leave them guessing what you want.
7. **Follow up within 24 hours.** Send the one-pager and a WhatsApp message.
