# JG Mart — Partner Pitch Script
## For: Krishi Market Wholesale Vendors
## Duration: 5 minutes
## Tone: Collaborative, not salesy

---

## Opening (30 seconds)
"Hi, I'm Fahad. I live in Japan Garden City — the big apartment complex about 800m from here. I've been buying from this market for years, and I know the quality you sell is way better than what people get at retail shops across the city.

I'm building a delivery service for JGC — 1,700 families, 27 buildings. We deliver fresh produce directly from Krishi Market to their doorsteps, same day.

I'm not here to compete with you. I'm here to bring you 1,700 guaranteed daily customers."

## The Problem You Face (1 minute)
"I've talked to a lot of vendors here. The biggest challenges are:
1. **Inconsistent volume** — some days you sell out by 10AM, some days you're stuck with leftover stock
2. **Retail middlemen** — the guys who buy from you and mark up 30-40% before selling to consumers
3. **Cash flow** — waiting 15-30 days for retail shops to pay you

What if you could sell directly to 1,700 families every single day, with guaranteed minimum orders?"

## The Solution (1 minute)
"JG Mart is a delivery platform. Here's how it works for you:

**1. We place orders every morning by 7AM**
You know exactly what we need, when we need it. No surprises.

**2. We pick up from your shop by 8AM**
Our riders come to you. You pack, we carry. No delivery hassle.

**3. We deliver to 27 buildings by 12PM**
Customers get fresh produce. You get paid immediately.

**4. We handle the money**
We pay you net-7 days. No waiting 30 days for retail shop collections."

## What You Get (1 minute)
"Here's what's in it for you:

**Guaranteed volume:** 1,700 families, multiple orders per day. Even on slow market days, you have us.

**Better prices for them, same for you:** We sell at wholesale prices (no retail markup). Your margins stay the same.

**No middleman:** We buy directly from you. No retail shop taking 30-40% cut.

**Fast payment:** Net-7 days, not net-30. Better cash flow.

**Growth:** As we expand to other compounds, your customer base grows with us."

## What We Need (1 minute)
"We're not asking for exclusivity. We're not asking for lower prices.

What we need is:
1. **Consistent quality** — same as what you sell to your regular customers
2. **Reliable timing** — orders ready by 8AM for morning delivery
3. **Transparent pricing** — wholesale rates, no hidden fees

That's it. We handle everything else."

## Next Steps (30 seconds)
"Can we start with a 2-week trial?
- You supply 10-15 products
- We deliver to 5 buildings (about 300 families)
- You get paid net-7
- If it works, we scale to all 27 buildings

If it doesn't work, no hard feelings. You keep the products, we keep the learnings.

Let's do this together."

---

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "What if you fail?" | We're funded for 6 months. Even if we don't scale, you get paid for 2 weeks of guaranteed orders. Zero risk. |
| "Why not sell directly to customers yourself?" | We're a delivery company, not a retail shop. You own the produce. We just move it. We don't compete — we amplify. |
| "What about quality complaints?" | We stand behind every order. If a customer complains, we handle it. You don't deal with returns. |
| "Can you guarantee minimum orders?" | Yes — we'll commit to ৳5,000/day minimum from you during the trial. If we don't hit it, we pay the difference. |
