# JG Mart — JGC Building Committee Pitch Script
## For: JGC Apartment Committee / Resident Welfare Association
## Duration: 3 minutes
## Tone: Community-focused, zero burden on committee

---

## Opening (30 seconds)
"Good morning/afternoon. I'm Fahad, resident of Building 7, Block C. I've been living here for [X] years.

I want to propose something that will make life easier for every family in JGC — with zero cost to the committee, zero hassle for you, and zero risk."

## The Problem (45 seconds)
"Every morning, I see my neighbors struggling with the same thing:
- Waking up early to go to the market
- Carrying heavy bags up the stairs
- Paying more than they should for vegetables
- Wasting time that could be spent with family

Last month alone, I heard at least 20 complaints about grocery delivery — late arrivals, wrong items, wilted vegetables.

There's a better way."

## The Solution (1 minute)
"I'm building JG Mart — a delivery service that brings fresh groceries from Krishi Market directly to each building in JGC.

**How it works for residents:**
1. Open WhatsApp, send a message to our number
2. Select what they want from our catalog
3. We deliver to their building by 12PM (morning) or 8PM (evening)
4. They pay cash on delivery

**What it means for the committee:**
- Zero cost
- Zero work
- Zero liability
- Happier residents

That's it. You don't have to do anything. We handle everything."

## Benefits for JGC (45 seconds)
"This isn't just about groceries. This is about community:

**Convenience:** Residents get fresh produce without leaving the building.

**Cost savings:** Wholesale prices — 30-40% cheaper than retail shops.

**Trust:** Only verified JG Mart riders enter the building. We know every flat, every resident.

**No disruption:** Deliveries happen at designated times. No clutter, no noise.

**Community feel:** We're JGC residents serving JGC residents. Not some outside company."

## What We Need (30 seconds)
"All we need from the committee is:
1. **Permission** to make deliveries to the buildings
2. **A contact person** — someone we can coordinate with if needed
3. **A notice** in the building WhatsApp group — just to let people know we exist

That's it. We handle everything else — delivery, payments, customer service."

## Pilot Proposal (30 seconds)
"I'm proposing a 2-week pilot:
- Free delivery for all buildings
- Limited to 100 orders to test the system
- If it works, we scale to all 1,700 families
- If it doesn't, we walk away with no hard feelings

The pilot costs us nothing. It costs you nothing. Residents get free groceries for 2 weeks."

## Closing (30 seconds)
"I'm not asking for money. I'm not asking for space. I'm asking for permission to make JGC a better place to live.

Every family deserves fresh, affordable groceries delivered to their door. Let's make that happen together.

Questions?"

---

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "What about security?" | Only verified riders enter. We have resident ID, photo, and building access logged. No strangers. |
| "What if deliveries clutter the lobby?" | Deliveries happen at specific times. We coordinate with building staff. No chaos. |
| "What about complaints?" | We handle all customer service. The committee doesn't deal with complaints. |
| "Why not let residents order directly?" | They can — via WhatsApp. We just make it easier with a catalog, delivery, and quality guarantee. |
| "What's in it for the committee?" | Happier residents, better community reputation, zero cost. You look good. |
