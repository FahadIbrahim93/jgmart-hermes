## 5. OBJECTION HANDLING CHEAT SHEET

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Why not just use Chaldal?" | Chaldal charges ৳40-60 delivery. We charge ৳0-30. We source from Krishi Market, not imported goods. We're 30-50% cheaper. |
| "What about cold chain?" | Bangladesh ambient temperatures are fine for 4-6 hour delivery. We optimize routes for speed, not refrigeration. |
| "What if Krishi Market raises prices?" | We have 5+ vendors at the market. If one raises, we switch. Our advantage is density, not single-vendor lock-in. |
| "Can you scale beyond JGC?" | Yes — the 27-building cluster is our MVP. Once we prove unit economics, we replicate to Gulshan, Banani, Dhanmondi. |
| "Why 250K? Why not less?" | 250K gives us 6 months of runway to hit 1,000 customers. Less capital means we run out before proving the model. |
| "What's your moat?" | Three layers: (1) Krishi Market sourcing relationships, (2) 4-cluster delivery optimization, (3) 1,700-family WhatsApp community. Hard to replicate. |
| "Who's your team?" | Founder lives in JGC. Operations partner at Krishi Market. Rider team of 3. We're lean, local, and embedded. |
