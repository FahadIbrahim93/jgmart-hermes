# JG Mart — Q&A Cheat Sheet

---

## General Questions

### Q: What makes you different from Chaldal?
**A:** Three things: (1) We source from Krishi Market, 800m away — wholesale prices, not imported retail markup. (2) We're hyperlocal — one compound, one community, one delivery route. No wasted mileage. (3) We use WhatsApp — no app download, no onboarding friction. Chaldal charges ৳40-60 delivery. We charge ৳0-30.

### Q: Why JGC? Why not start with a bigger market?
**A:** JGC is our beachhead — 1,700 families, 27 buildings, 4 clusters. It's large enough to prove unit economics, small enough to dominate. If we nail JGC, we replicate to Gulshan, Banani, Dhanmondi. Starting big is a recipe for failure. Starting focused lets us learn fast.

### Q: How do you make money?
**A:** Three revenue streams: (1) Product markup — we buy at wholesale, sell at 20-30% markup. (2) Delivery fees — ৳0-30 per order. (3) Subscription — ৳20/month for unlimited free delivery. Our 51% contribution margin means every order pays for itself plus overhead.

### Q: What's your 250K BDT ask for?
**A:** Six months of runway to hit 1,000 active customers and prove profitability. Breakdown: 50K technology, 75K marketing, 75K working capital, 50K operations. If we hit our milestones, we don't need more money — we're profitable. If we don't, you know in 6 months, not 18.

### Q: What's your moat?
**A:** Three layers: (1) Krishi Market sourcing relationships — you can't replicate 5 years of vendor trust in 6 months. (2) 4-cluster delivery optimization — we know every building, every flat, every slot. (3) 1,700-family WhatsApp community — trust and word-of-mouth in a gated compound are powerful.

### Q: Who's your team?
**A:** Me — founder, lives in JGC, built the entire platform solo. Operations partner — 5+ years at Krishi Market, knows every vendor. 3 riders — JGC residents, trusted by the community. We're lean, local, and embedded.

### Q: What's the exit?
**A:** Three paths: (1) Organic profitability — we hit ৳500K/month revenue, take home ৳250K profit, live happily ever after. (2) Acquisition — Chaldal, Shwapno, or a logistics company buys us for our community + route density. (3) Series A — we expand to 5-10 compounds, raise ৳5-10M, scale to 50K+ families.

## Investor-Specific Questions

### Q: What's your burn rate?
**A:** ~৳40K/month — 20K operations (riders, fuel), 15K marketing, 5K technology. 250K gives us 6 months.

### Q: What's your customer acquisition cost?
**A:** Currently ৳50-100 per customer via flyers + WhatsApp referrals. Referral program brings it down to ৳30. Target: ৳20 by Month 3.

### Q: What's your churn rate?
**A:** Early data shows <5% monthly churn. Once someone orders once, they order again — fresh produce is a recurring need. Our referral program compounds retention.

### Q: How do you handle returns/refunds?
**A:** WhatsApp-based. Customer sends photo of bad produce, we refund or replace within 24 hours. No complicated process. We eat the cost — quality is our brand.

### Q: What if Krishi Market raises prices?
**A:** We have 5+ vendors at the market. If one raises, we switch. Our advantage is density, not single-vendor lock-in. We also plan to partner directly with farms for 2-3 high-volume items.

## Partner-Specific Questions (Krishi Market Vendors)

### Q: Why should I work with you instead of selling to retail shops?
**A:** Retail shops pay net-30, take 30-40% margin, and only buy small quantities. We pay net-7, take zero margin on your goods, and buy in bulk for 1,700 families. More volume, faster payment, better relationship.

### Q: What if you fail?
**A:** We're funded for 6 months. Even if we don't scale, you get 2 weeks of guaranteed orders. Zero risk. If it works, you grow with us.

### Q: Can I sell to other delivery services too?
**A:** Yes — we don't ask for exclusivity. We just ask for consistent quality and reliable timing. You're free to sell to anyone.

### Q: What about quality standards?
**A:** We hold ourselves to the same standard you hold for your regular customers. If a customer complains, we handle it. We don't return bad produce to you — we replace it from our own stock.

## Committee-Specific Questions (JGC Building Committee)

### Q: What's in it for the committee?
**A:** Happier residents, better community reputation, zero cost. You don't have to do anything — we handle all deliveries, payments, and complaints. You just get to tell residents "yes, we partnered with JG Mart for grocery delivery."

### Q: What about security?
**A:** Only verified JG Mart riders enter the building. Each rider has a photo ID, resident verification, and delivery log. No strangers, no chaos.

### Q: What if deliveries clutter the lobby?
**A:** We deliver at specific times (9AM-12PM, 3:30PM-8PM). We coordinate with building staff. If the lobby is an issue, we set up a delivery station at the gate.

### Q: Can residents opt out?
**A:** Absolutely. This is opt-in. No one is forced to use JG Mart. If a resident doesn't want deliveries, we don't deliver to them.

### Q: What about complaints?
**A:** We handle all customer service. The committee doesn't deal with complaints, returns, or issues. We have a direct WhatsApp line for residents to reach us.

## Tough Questions

### Q: What's your biggest risk?
**A:** Three risks: (1) Quality inconsistency — we mitigate with daily Krishi Market sourcing and rider training. (2) Delivery delays — we mitigate with 4 optimized clusters and backup riders. (3) Low adoption — we mitigate with free delivery week and referral bonuses.

### Q: What if a bigger player enters the market?
**A:** We welcome competition — it validates the market. But Chaldal can't match our prices (they have dark store costs). Shwapno can't match our speed (they have retail overhead). Our moat is structural: 800m from Krishi Market + 1,700 families in one compound.

### Q: How do you scale beyond JGC?
**A:** The 27-building cluster is our MVP. Once we prove unit economics here, we replicate the playbook: find a compound near a wholesale market, onboard 5-10 buildings, optimize the route, scale. Each expansion is a copy-paste of the JGC model.

### Q: What if the building committee says no?
**A:** We start with 5 buildings (one cluster) and prove the model. Once residents see the value, the committee can't say no. We let demand drive adoption, not top-down permission.
