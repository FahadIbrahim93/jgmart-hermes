The Problem

1.7 million families in Dhaka pay retail prices for vegetables that cost 30% less at wholesale. The gap isn't supply — it's logistics.

Every morning, 1,700 families in JGC face the same choice:

  • Wake up at 6AM, haggle at the market, carry heavy bags up 4 flights of stairs
  • Pay 30-50% markup at the local retail shop
  • Order online, wait 2-3 days, get wilted produce delivered by strangers
  • Skip fresh vegetables and survive on packaged food

Nobody offers wholesale prices + same-day delivery + personal trust.
