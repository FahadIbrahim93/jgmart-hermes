## The Problem
Dhaka apartment dwellers pay 30-50% retail markup on vegetables that cost 30% less at wholesale. The gap isn't supply — it's logistics. No one offers wholesale prices + same-day delivery + personal trust for gated communities.

## The Solution
- **Sourcing:** Daily from Krishi Market (800m from JGC)
- **Delivery:** Morning (order by 9AM → 12PM) / Evening (order by 3:30PM → 8PM)
- **Pricing:** Free delivery for 13 buildings, ৳20/30 for others
- **Ordering:** WhatsApp in 30 seconds — no app download
