How to Order
Ordering groceries from JG Mart is as easy as 1-2-3-4.
1

📖 Browse the Catalog

Explore 65+ products in our online catalog — fresh vegetables, fish, meat, rice, spices, dairy, and more. Updated daily with Krishi Market prices.

2

➕ Add Items to Cart

Select what you need. The cart automatically calculates your total and delivery fee based on your building. Review before sending.

3

💬 Send via WhatsApp

Tap the WhatsApp button to send your order. Fill in your name, building, flat number, and preferred slot. We'll confirm within minutes.

4

🚚 We Deliver to Your Door

We head to Krishi Market, hand-pick everything fresh, and deliver to your flat within your chosen slot. Pay cash or bKash on arrival.
