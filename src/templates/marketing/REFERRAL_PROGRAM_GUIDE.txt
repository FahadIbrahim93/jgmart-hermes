1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW
================================================================================

The JG Mart Referral Program rewards our existing customers for bringing new
neighbours and friends into the JG Mart community. Every time a referred
customer places their first order (minimum 300 BDT), both the referrer and
the referred customer receive 100 BDT credit toward their next purchase.

This program is designed to:
- Grow our customer base organically through trusted word-of-mouth.
- Reward loyal customers who advocate for JG Mart.
- Build community within Japan Garden City buildings and surrounding areas.

================================================================================
2. PROGRAM RULES
================================================================================

2.1 Eligibility
- Any existing JG Mart customer who has placed at least one order is eligible
  to refer others.
- The referred person must be a NEW customer — never ordered from JG Mart before.
- JG Mart employees and their immediate family members are not eligible to
  participate.

2.2 Referral Credit (100 BDT Per Successful Referral)
- The referring customer receives 100 BDT credit when the referred customer's
  first order is completed and delivered.
- The referred customer also receives 100 BDT credit on their first order
  (applied automatically as a discount or added as credit after delivery).
- First order must be minimum 300 BDT to qualify.
- Credit is non-transferable and can only be used on JG Mart orders.

2.3 Monthly Cap
- Maximum 500 BDT in referral credits per referrer per calendar month.
- This equals a maximum of 5 successful referrals per month.
- The cap resets on the 1st of each month.
- Any referrals beyond the cap will still count, but the credit is paid out
  in the following month (subject to that month's cap).

2.4 One Code Per Referral
- Each referral must use one unique referral code on their first order.
- If a referred customer tries to apply multiple codes, only the first one
  applied will be honoured.

2.5 Code Validity
- Referral codes do not expire — they remain active as long as the referring
  customer remains an active JG Mart customer.
- However, the CREDIT earned from a referral expires 90 days after issue
  (see Section 6).

3. HOW CUSTOMERS SHARE THEIR CODE
================================================================================

Each customer receives a unique referral code when they place their first
order. The code is sent to them via WhatsApp confirmation message.

3.1 Sharing Methods

  A) WhatsApp Forward (Recommended)
     - Customer opens their WhatsApp chat with JG Mart.
     - They tap the referral message and select "Forward."
     - They send it to friends, family, or building groups.
     - The forwarded message includes their unique code and a short blurb.

  B) Building WhatsApp Groups
     - Customers can share their code in their building's community group.
     - Suggested message templates are in Section 8 below.
     - We encourage building admins to share on behalf of residents.

  C) In-Person / Word of Mouth
     - Customer tells their neighbour verbally and shares the code.
     - The neighbour enters the code when placing their first order.
     - Works well for same-building referrals.

  D) Social Media (Optional)
     - Customers may share on Facebook groups or community pages.
     - Only the unique code is shared — no personal contact info.

3.2 Referral Code Format
     Format: JGC-XXXXX (e.g., JGC-RAF001, JGC-NAS002)
     The first three letters are always "JGC" for JG Mart.
     The next three letters are the first three letters of the customer's name.
     The last three digits are a sequential number.

4. HOW WE TRACK AND CREDIT
================================================================================

4.1 Tracking Process

  Step 1: Customer places first order with a referral code.
  Step 2: Order team spots the referral code in the order notes or customer
          account.
  Step 3: Team opens REFERRAL_TRACKER.csv (located in 12_Templates/).
  Step 4: Fill in:
          - Referral Date (date code was shared or first order date)
          - First Order Date (actual first order completion date)
          - First Order Value (total order amount in BDT)
          - Referrer Credit Earned (100 BDT per successful referral)
          - Status → "Active"
  Step 5: Message the referrer: "Great news! Your referral [Name] just placed
          their first order. You've earned 100 BDT credit!"
  Step 6: Message the referred customer: "Welcome to JG Mart! You've earned
          100 BDT credit for your first order. Use it on your next purchase!"

4.2 Credit Application
  - Credits are applied as a discount code applied to the customer's account.
  - When the customer places their next order and asks to use credit, the team
    deducts from the Credit Balance column in the tracker.
  - Update: Credit Redeemed + new Credit Balance, and change Status to
    "Redeemed" when fully used.

4.3 Monthly Reconciliation
  - On the 1st of each month, review all Active credits.
  - Calculate total credits earned per referrer to enforce the 500 BDT cap.
  - Flag any referrers approaching or exceeding the cap.
  - Credits that exceed the cap roll to the next month.

4.4 Expiration Check (Every 90 Days)
  - Run a check every week: compare Referral Date + 90 days against today.
  - Any credit older than 90 days that has not been redeemed → Status = "Expired".
  - Message the customer: "Your 100 BDT referral credit from [Date] has
    expired. Don't worry — you can earn new credits by referring someone new!"

================================================================================
5. MONTHLY CAP
================================================================================

Purpose: Prevent abuse and ensure the program remains financially sustainable.

Rules:
- Maximum credit a referrer can earn: 500 BDT per calendar month.
- Soft cap warning at 400 BDT (4 referrals) — the team proactively messages
  the customer letting them know they're approaching the limit.
- Hard cap enforced at 500 BDT (5 referrals) — no further credits issued
  that month. Excess referrals roll to the next month.
- The cap resets to 0 on the 1st of every month at 00:00.

Example:
  - Rafiq refers 6 people in May → earns 500 BDT in May (cap hit).
  - The 6th referral's 100 BDT rolls to June.
  - In June, Rafiq can earn up to 500 BDT again. The rolled-over 100 BDT
    counts against June's cap.

Exception: The cap may be temporarily increased during promotional periods
(e.g., launch week, anniversary sale) at management's discretion. Any
promotional change must be announced to all customers in advance.

================================================================================
6. EXPIRATION POLICY
================================================================================

- Referral credits expire 90 (ninety) calendar days after they are earned.
- The expiration date is calculated from the "First Order Date" of the
  referred customer.
- Expired credits are forfeited and cannot be reinstated.
- The team sends a reminder message 7 days before expiry:
    "Your 100 BDT referral credit will expire in 7 days. Use it on your
     next order before [date]!"
- Once expired, the Status column is updated to "Expired" and the credit
  balance is set to 0 in the tracker.

7. FAQ
================================================================================

Q1: What if my referral never orders?
A: That's okay! Not everyone will order right away. The credit is only earned
   when the referred person places and completes their first order (minimum
   300 BDT). You can share your code with as many people as you like — you'll
   only be credited for those who actually order. There's no penalty for
   referrals that don't convert.

Q2: Can I refer myself?
A: No. The referred customer must be a NEW customer who has never ordered from
   JG Mart before. Referring yourself (using your own code on a second account,
   asking family members in the same household to use your code, etc.) violates
   the program rules and may result in forfeiture of all credits and removal
   from the program.

Q3: What if someone uses multiple referral codes?
A: Only the FIRST referral code applied to a new customer's first order will
   be honoured. Our order team checks for this during processing. If a customer
   tries to enter multiple codes, we use the earliest one and disregard the
   rest. The customer is informed that only one code could be applied.

Q4: Can I refer someone in the same building as me?
A: Yes! Same-building referrals are encouraged. Many of our strongest
   referrals happen between neighbours. The program works exactly the same —
   you still earn 100 BDT when they place their first order.

Q5: Can I refer someone who lives in a different building or area?
A: Absolutely. As long as the referred person is within our delivery zone
   (Japan Garden City and surrounding areas), they are eligible.

Q6: How do I know how much credit I have?
A: You can ask us anytime via WhatsApp. We'll check the tracker and tell you
   your current credit balance. We also send updates when you earn credit,
   when you use it, and when it's about to expire.

Q7: Can I combine referral credit with other discounts or promotions?
A: Referral credit cannot be combined with other promotional discounts on the
   same order unless explicitly stated. However, you can use referral credit
   on one order and a promotion on another — there's no limit to how often you
   can order.

Q8: What happens to my referral code if I stop ordering from JG Mart?
A: If an account has been inactive for 6+ months (no orders), the referral
   code is deactivated. The customer would need to place a new order to
   reactivate their code.

Q9: Does the referred customer also get 100 BDT?
A: Yes! Both the referrer AND the referred customer receive 100 BDT credit.
   The referred customer's credit is applied automatically to their first
   order (as a discount) or added as post-delivery credit.

Q10: How do I use my credit?
A: Simply tell our order team via WhatsApp or phone when placing your next
    order: "I'd like to use my referral credit." We'll check your balance and
    apply it. Credits can be used in full or in part.

9. TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

9.1 Referral Code Not Working
- Check for typos in the code (letters vs numbers, case-sensitive?).
- Confirm the referred customer is actually new (never ordered before).
- Verify the code hasn't been deactivated due to referrer inactivity.
- Solution: Manually apply the code from the Operations Dashboard if system
  issue is confirmed.

9.2 Credit Not Showing After Referral Order
- Ensure the referred customer's first order met the 300 BDT minimum.
- Check that the order was completed and delivered (not cancelled/refunded).
- Confirm the referrer hasn't hit the 500 BDT monthly cap.
- If all checks pass, manually add the credit and update the tracker.

9.3 Customer Claims They Referred Someone But Didn't Get Credit
- Ask for the referred customer's name and order date.
- Check the tracker for the referral code.
- If the code wasn't applied at order time, investigate whether the referred
  customer mentioned it verbally.
- If it was a genuine miss, credit retroactively and apologise.
- If no evidence, politely explain the policy and encourage them to ensure
  the code is shared at order time next time.

9.4 Dispute: "I referred them before, why isn't it showing?"
- Check if the code was used on an earlier order that was cancelled.
- Check if the referred customer had previously ordered without a code
  (making them ineligible as a "new" customer).
- Check for duplicate entries in the tracker.
- Resolve by referring to the First Order Date in tracker history.
