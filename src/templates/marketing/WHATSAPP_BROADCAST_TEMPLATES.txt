BROADCAST RULES (MANDATORY)
----------------------------
  1. Only send to customers who have explicitly opted in.
  2. Use broadcast lists — never create groups without permission.
  3. Include an opt-out instruction in every message (see footer template).
  4. Keep messages under 300 words for readability.
  5. Send between 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM Bangladesh time only.
  6. Personalize with the customer’s first name when possible.

TEMPLATE 5 — WEATHER DELAY NOTIFICATION
================================================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Proactively inform customers about delivery delays due to rain, strikes,
traffic, or other disruptions. Transparency builds trust.

HEAVY RAIN / MONSOON TEMPLATE:
  🌧️ Heads Up, [Customer First Name]!

  Heavy rain across Dhaka today. Our riders are out, but deliveries may
  be delayed by 30–60 minutes.

  We’re prioritizing:
  ✅ Essential grocery orders (vegetables, milk, bread)
  ⏳ Non-essential / snack orders may be slightly delayed

  Safety first — our riders will not ride in dangerous conditions. We
  appreciate your patience.

  If you need to reschedule, reply "RESCHEDULE" and we’ll move your
  order to tomorrow morning.

  Stay dry! 🌂
  — Team JG Mart

TRAFFIC / STRIKE TEMPLATE:
  🚧 Delivery Notice: Traffic Disruption

  Due to [reason: road work / strike / major event] near [location],
  deliveries in [affected zones] may be delayed by 45–90 minutes today.

  Affected areas: [Zone A], [Zone B]
  Unaffected areas: [Zone C], [Zone D] (delivering on time)

  We’re sorry for the inconvenience. If you can’t wait, reply "CANCEL"
  and we’ll issue a full refund immediately.

  Thanks for understanding!
  — Team JG Mart
