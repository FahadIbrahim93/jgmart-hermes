---------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW
---------------------------------------------------------------------

OBJECTIVE
---------
Build trust, drive daily orders, and grow the JG Mart community across
Japan Garden City and neighboring clusters. Content should feel local,
fresh, and genuinely useful—not like a faceless brand talking at people.

CONTENT PILLARS
---------------
1. PRODUCT PHOTOS       Fresh produce, prices, daily catalog highlights
2. CUSTOMER TESTIMONIALS Real JGC residents sharing their experiences
3. BEHIND-THE-SCENES    Krishi Market sourcing, packing, delivery rides
4. DELIVERY UPDATES     Real-time zone updates, peak-hour alerts
5. REFERRAL REMINDERS   "Refer a neighbor" campaigns and success stories
6. COMMUNITY SPOTLIGHTS Local JGC events, block initiatives, team features

---------------------------------------------------------------------
BEST POSTING TIMES (BANGLADESH TIMEZONE — BST)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  MONDAY – FRIDAY
  ----------------
    7:30 AM   — Morning catalog peek (highest engagement)
    12:30 PM  — Lunch-break scroll (office workers, students)
    7:00 PM   — Evening dinner-planning window

  SATURDAY – SUNDAY
  -----------------
    9:00 AM   — Weekend grocery planning
    1:00 PM   — Family afternoon browsing
    8:00 PM   — Evening recap / next-day preview

  RAMADAN / EID
  ------------
    4:30 PM   — Iftar planning window
    10:00 PM  — Sehri / late-night grocery needs

  PEAK AVOIDANCE
  --------------
    Avoid posting between 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM on weekdays (low engagement).
