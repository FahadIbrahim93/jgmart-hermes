---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DOCUMENT OVERVIEW
---------------------------------------------------------------------

DOCUMENT:        JG Mart A5 Flyer — Front & Back
PURPOSE:         Door-to-door distribution, community notice boards, cafe counters
TARGET AUDIENCE: JGC residents, tower security desks, local shopkeepers
PRINT RUN:       2,000 – 5,000 copies (initial run)

---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DIMENSIONS & BLEED
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Finished Size:    148mm × 210mm (A5)
  Bleed:            3mm on all sides
  Safe Zone:        5mm from trim edge on all sides
  Total Artboard:   154mm × 216mm (including bleed)

  FOLDING:          None (flat A5). If folding to DL is required later,
                    add center fold guide at 74mm.

  COLOUR MODE:      CMYK (no RGB for print)
  RESOLUTION:       300 DPI minimum for all images and graphics
  FILE FORMAT:      PDF/X-1a or PDF/X-4 with bleed
  FONTS:            Outlined or embedded (all Bangla + English fonts licensed)

---------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FRONT SIDE — DESIGN LAYOUT
---------------------------------------------------------------------

HEADLINE ZONE (Top 35% of canvas)
----------------------------------

CALL-TO-ACTION ZONE (Bottom 25% of canvas)
------------------------------------------
  QR Code:        150mm × 150mm (scaled to ~40mm × 40mm on print)
                  Links to: WhatsApp chat, website, or order form
                  Place: Centered or right-aligned

                  "Delivering to: JGC Block A, B, C | Tower 1, 2, 3 |

                  Free delivery on orders over ৳800."

5. PRINT SPECIFICATIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  COLOUR:         CMYK only. Convert all brand colors:
                  Primary Green:   C78 M0 Y65 K28
                  Secondary Green: C72 M0 Y60 K0
                  White:           C0 M0 Y0 K0
                  Dark Gray:       C70 M60 Y60 K40

  PAPER TYPE:     100gsm coated art paper (preferred)
                  OR 80gsm uncoated offset paper (budget option)
                  RECOMMENDED: 128gsm coated for premium feel

  FINISH:         Gloss lamination on front (protects hero image)
                  Matte lamination on back (writes on referral section)
                  ALTERNATIVE: Aqueous coating on both sides (cost-effective)

  BLEED SETUP:    3mm bleed on all sides in InDesign / Illustrator
                  Use slug area for printer notes, crop marks, registration

  FONTS:          Outline all text or embed licensed fonts.
                  Bangla fonts: Noto Sans Bengali or Kalpurush
                  English fonts: Inter, Poppins

  IMAGE RES:      All photos must be 300 DPI at final print size.
                  Minimum 10MB for hero images. Avoid screen grabs.

  PROOFING:       Request a PDF soft proof before final print run.
                  Check color accuracy, QR code scannability, and bleed.

6. QR CODE SPECIFICATIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  FRONT QR CODE:
    Purpose:      Link to WhatsApp chat or order page
    Size:         40mm × 40mm (print), 200px × 200px (digital mockup)
    Error Corr.:  Medium (M) — 15% damage tolerance
    Quiet Zone:   4mm white border around QR code
    Test:         Scan with 3 different phones before sending to print

  BACK QR CODE:
    Purpose:      Direct WhatsApp link with pre-filled message
    URL Format:   https://wa.me/8801XXXXXXXXX?text=Hi%20JG%20Mart,%20I%20want%20to%20order
    Size:         35mm × 35mm
    Error Corr.:  Medium (M)
    Quiet Zone:   4mm white border

7. DISTRIBUTION STRATEGY
---------------------------------------------------------------------

7.1 DISTRIBUTION LOCATIONS
---------------------------
  Priority 1 (High Foot Traffic):
    • JGC main gate security desk
    • Each tower lobby / reception
    • JGC community center
    • Near elevators on ground and first floors
    • Local grocery stores and tea stalls inside JGC

  Priority 2 (Secondary):
    • Nearby apartments outside JGC but within delivery radius
    • Mosque notice boards (especially before Ramadan/Eid)
    • JGC park / playground bulletin board
    • Local pharmacy counters
    • Rickshaw and CNG stands near JGC entrances

7.2 DISTRIBUTION METHOD
------------------------
  Door-to-door:   Security guards or JG Mart brand ambassadors drop flyers
                  at every apartment door during [time window: 5–7 PM].
                  Wear JG Mart branded t-shirts. Carry a clipboard for
                  interested residents to write their numbers.

  Counter Drop:   Leave stacks of 50–100 flyers at each location listed
                  above. Rotate weekly to prevent theft or hoarding.

  Event Handout:  Distribute at JGC block tournaments, Eid gatherings,
                  and community meetings.

7.3 QUANTITY & FREQUENCY
-------------------------
  Initial run:    5,000 copies (A5 front + back)
  Re-print cycle: Monthly or when stock falls below 500
  Seasonal bump:  Increase to 8,000 during Ramadan, Eid, and exam seasons

7.4 TRACKING EFFECTIVENESS
---------------------------
  Unique referral code printed on each flyer: JGFLYER-[AREA]
  Example codes:
    JGFLYER-JGC-A   (for JGC Block A drops)
    JGFLYER-JGC-B   (for JGC Block B drops)
    JGFLYER-MOSQUE  (for mosque distributions)

  Track redemptions by code to measure ROI per location.
