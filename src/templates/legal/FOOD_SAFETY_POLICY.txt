================================================================================
                    1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
================================================================================

1.1  PURPOSE. The purpose of this Food Safety Policy is to establish
     standards, procedures, and responsibilities for ensuring that all
     grocery items, fresh produce, and food products supplied, handled,
     and delivered by JG Mart meet the highest standards of safety,
     quality, and hygiene.

1.2  SCOPE. This policy applies to:
     - All JG Mart employees, riders, and contractors;
     - All partner shops and suppliers sourcing from Krishi Market and
       other approved vendors;
     - All stages of the supply chain: sourcing, sorting, packaging,
       and delivery;
     - All products handled by JG Mart, including fresh produce, dairy,
       meat, fish, packaged foods, and household items.

1.3  OBJECTIVES. JG Mart is committed to:
     - Protecting the health and safety of our customers;
     - Maintaining the quality and freshness of all products;
     - Complying with all applicable food safety laws and regulations
       in Bangladesh;
     - Building trust and confidence in the JG Mart brand;
     - Continuously improving our food safety and quality assurance
       systems.

================================================================================
                    2. APPLICABLE STANDARDS AND LAWS
================================================================================

JG Mart shall comply with all applicable food safety laws, regulations,
and standards, including but not limited to:

2.1  Bangladesh Food Safety Act, [YEAR] and regulations promulgated
     thereunder;

2.2  Bangladesh Standards and Testing Institution (BSTI) standards;

2.3  Bangladesh Agricultural Research Council (BARC) guidelines for
     fresh produce;

2.4  Department of Agricultural Extension (DAE) recommendations;

2.5  Local government (City Corporation) health and sanitation
     regulations;

2.6  International standards where applicable (Codex Alimentarius,
     HACCP principles);

2.7  Any other applicable laws, rules, notifications, or guidelines
     issued by relevant authorities.

================================================================================
                    3. SOURCING STANDARDS
================================================================================

3.1  APPROVED SUPPLIERS. All fresh produce and grocery items shall be
     sourced from approved suppliers, with a primary focus on Krishi
     Market and other verified partner shops that meet JG Mart's quality
     and safety standards. No product shall be sourced from unapproved
     or unverified vendors.

3.2  SUPPLIER APPROVAL CRITERIA. Before a supplier is approved, JG Mart
     shall verify:
     - Valid trade license and food license / food hygiene permit;
     - Compliance with BSTI and Bangladesh Food Safety Authority
       standards;
     - Physical inspection of storage and handling facilities;
     - Product quality and freshness standards;
     - Traceability and documentation practices;
     - History of compliance and any prior violations.

3.3  SUPPLIER MONITORING. Approved suppliers shall be monitored
     regularly through:
     - Periodic facility inspections (at least quarterly);
     - Random product sampling and quality testing;
     - Review of supplier documentation and certifications;
     - Customer feedback and complaint analysis;
     - Mystery shopping and spot checks.

3.4  PROHIBITED SOURCES. Products shall not be sourced from:
     - Vendors operating without valid licenses;
     - Vendors with a history of food safety violations;
     - Unregulated or informal markets (unless specifically approved
       for a particular product category);
     - Sources known to supply adulterated, contaminated, or substandard
       goods.

3.5  TRACEABILITY. All products shall be traceable to their source.
     Suppliers shall maintain records of:
     - Origin of produce (farm, market, or supplier);
     - Date of harvest or procurement;
     - Batch or lot numbers (where applicable);
     - Storage and handling conditions;
     - Expiry or "best before" dates.

================================================================================
                    4. QUALITY CHECKS AT THREE STAGES
================================================================================

JG Mart shall conduct quality checks at three critical stages:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                    STAGE 1: PICKUP (Supplier Premises)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1.1 VISUAL INSPECTION. At the time of pickup from the partner shop or
       supplier, JG Mart staff or riders shall conduct a visual inspection
       of all items to verify:
       - Product freshness and quality (no visible spoilage, bruising,
         wilting, or discoloration);
       - Correct product type and variety;
       - Quantity matches the Order;
       - Packaging is intact and appropriate;
       - Labelling is correct and legible (batch number, expiry date,
         manufacturer details);
       - No signs of pest infestation, contamination, or damage.

4.1.2 REJECTION AT PICKUP. Any product failing the Stage 1 inspection
       shall be rejected immediately. The rejection shall be recorded in
       the Order notes, and the Partner shall be notified. Rejected items
       shall not be accepted for delivery.

4.1.3 TEMPERATURE CHECK. For temperature-sensitive products (dairy,
       meat, fish, frozen items), JG Mart staff shall verify that:
       - Cold chain has been maintained during storage and handling;
       - Products are stored at appropriate temperatures at the supplier
         premises;
       - Insulated packaging is used for transport.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                    STAGE 2: SORTING (JG Mart Warehouse / Hub)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.2.1 RECEIPT AND SORTING. Upon arrival at the JG Mart warehouse or
       sorting hub, all items shall be:
       - Logged into the inventory system with timestamp;
       - Sorted by product category, delivery route, and time slot;
       - Inspected again for any damage or deterioration during transit.

4.2.2 FRESH PRODUCE SORTING. Fresh fruits and vegetables shall be
       individually inspected and sorted:
       - Remove bruised, damaged, or overripe items;
       - Remove items with visible mold, rot, or pest damage;
       - Ensure uniformity of size and quality for customer satisfaction;
       - Re-sort into appropriate packaging for delivery.

4.2.3 REJECTION AT SORTING. Items failing the Stage 2 inspection shall
       be segregated and recorded. The affected Orders shall be updated,
       and customers shall be notified of any substitutions or removals.

4.2.4 PACKAGING AT SORTING HUB. All items shall be:
       - Repacked into JG Mart delivery bags with appropriate
         insulation/protection;
       - Labeled with customer name, Order number, and delivery address;
       - Organized by delivery route for efficient rider dispatch.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                    STAGE 3: DELIVERY (Final Customer Handover)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.3.1 PRE-DELIVERY CHECK. Before leaving the sorting hub, riders shall
       verify:
       - Order completeness (all items present, correctly packed);
       - Customer address and contact information;
       - Special handling instructions (fragile, keep cool, etc.).

4.3.2 DELIVERY INSPECTION. At the point of delivery:
       - Rider shall present the Order to the customer;
       - Customer may inspect items for visible damage or incorrect items;
       - Any discrepancies shall be noted on the receipt and reported
         immediately;
       - Customer shall sign or otherwise acknowledge receipt.

4.3.3 CUSTOMER FEEDBACK. Riders shall encourage customers to report any
       quality issues immediately. Customers may also report issues via
       WhatsApp within [INSERT PERIOD, e.g., 24 hours] of delivery.

4.3.4 FINAL RECORD. Delivery confirmation, including any customer
       complaints or quality issues, shall be recorded in the order
       management system for quality tracking and supplier feedback.

================================================================================
                    5. TEMPERATURE CONTROL
================================================================================

5.1  COLD CHAIN MANAGEMENT. For perishable products, JG Mart shall maintain
     an unbroken cold chain from supplier to customer:

     - Cold storage at supplier premises (verified during Stage 1);
     - Insulated delivery bags and cold packs during transit;
     - Priority delivery of cold chain items to minimize time out of
       refrigeration;

5.2  TEMPERATURE REQUIREMENTS. Maximum permissible temperatures for
     different product categories:

     - Dairy Products (milk, yogurt, cheese):    Refrigerated at 2-4C
     - Fresh Meat and Poultry:                   Refrigerated at 0-4C
     - Fresh Fish and Seafood:                   Refrigerated at 0-2C
     - Frozen Products:                          Frozen at -18C or below
     - Fresh Produce (fruits/vegetables):        Ambient (10-25C),
                                                  protected from heat

5.3  MONITORING. JG Mart shall:
     - Use temperature monitoring devices in cold storage (if applicable);
     - Conduct random temperature checks during sorting and delivery;
     - Reject any cold chain product that shows signs of temperature
       abuse (thawing, warming, condensation);

5.4  RECALL PROTOCOL. In the event of a temperature control failure or
     contamination incident affecting a batch of products, JG Mart shall
     immediately:
     - Identify affected Orders and customers;
     - Notify affected customers via WhatsApp or phone;
     - Arrange for collection or safe disposal of affected products;
     - Report the incident to relevant authorities if required by law.

================================================================================
                    6. INCIDENT REPORTING
================================================================================

6.1  INCIDENT CLASSIFICATION. Food safety incidents include:
     - Customer complaints of illness or adverse reaction after consuming
       JG Mart products;
     - Discovery of contamination, adulteration, or foreign objects in
       products;
     - Supplier notification of product recall or safety alert;
     - Temperature control failure affecting perishable goods;
     - Pest infestation or unsanitary conditions at supplier or JG Mart
       premises;
     - Delivery of expired, damaged, or substandard products.

6.2  REPORTING PROCEDURE. Any employee, rider, or partner who identifies
     or becomes aware of a food safety incident shall immediately report
     it to the Operations Manager or designated Food Safety Officer.

6.3  INCIDENT LOG. All incidents shall be recorded in the Incident Log,
     including:
     - Date and time of discovery;
     - Description of the incident;
     - Products and Orders affected;
     - Actions taken;
     - Follow-up measures;
     - Resolution status.

6.4  AUTHORITY NOTIFICATION. Where required by Applicable Law, JG Mart
     shall notify the Bangladesh Food Safety Authority, City Corporation
     health department, or other relevant authority within the timeframe
     specified by law.

================================================================================
                    7. CUSTOMER COMPLAINT HANDLING
================================================================================

7.1  COMPLAINT CHANNELS. Customers may report food quality or safety
     concerns via:
     - WhatsApp: [WHATSAPP NUMBER]
     - Phone: [PHONE NUMBER]
     - In-person at JG Mart office

7.2  COMPLAINT ACKNOWLEDGMENT. All complaints shall be acknowledged within
     [INSERT PERIOD, e.g., 2 hours] during business hours.

7.3  COMPLAINT CLASSIFICATION.
     - Minor: Cosmetic issues, packaging damage (no health risk);
     - Moderate: Product quality issues, wrong item, partial spoilage;
     - Serious: Suspected contamination, foreign objects, illness
       after consumption.

7.4  INVESTIGATION. Each complaint shall be investigated promptly:
     - Review Order details, delivery records, and supplier information;
     - Cross-reference with Stage 1, 2, and 3 quality checks;
     - Interview involved staff or riders if necessary;
     - Inspect remaining stock from the same batch or supplier.

7.5  RESOLUTION AND REMEDY.
     - Minor complaints: Apology, explanation, small goodwill gesture
       (discount, free item on next order);
     - Moderate complaints: Partial or full refund, replacement order,
       or credit;
     - Serious complaints: Full refund, medical support referral if
       needed, immediate supplier investigation and possible
       termination, authority notification if required.

7.6  RECORD KEEPING. All complaints shall be recorded in the Complaint
     Register, including:
     - Customer name and contact;
     - Order number and date;
     - Nature of complaint;
     - Action taken;
     - Outcome and resolution;
     - Follow-up (if any).

7.7  TREND ANALYSIS. The Operations Manager shall review the Complaint
     Register monthly to identify trends, recurring issues, or supplier
     problems. Corrective actions shall be implemented as needed.

================================================================================
                    9. STAFF TRAINING AND RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================

9.1  TRAINING REQUIREMENTS. All JG Mart employees and riders involved
     in food handling, sorting, packaging, or delivery shall receive
     food safety training:
     - Upon hiring (induction training);
     - At least annually (refresher training);
     - When new procedures or regulations are introduced.

9.2  TRAINING CONTENT. Training shall cover:
     - Basic food hygiene and sanitation;
     - Personal hygiene requirements (hand washing, clean clothing,
       no jewelry);
     - Cross-contamination prevention;
     - Temperature control and cold chain management;
     - Recognition of spoiled or unsafe food;
     - Cleaning and sanitizing procedures;
     - Pest control basics;
     - Incident reporting procedures;
     - Applicable food safety laws and regulations.

9.3  RESPONSIBILITIES.
     - Founder / Operations Manager: Overall responsibility for food
       safety policy and compliance;
     - Food Safety Officer (if designated): Day-to-day oversight,
       training coordination, incident management;
     - Warehouse / Sorting Staff: Stage 2 quality checks, sorting,
       packaging, temperature control;
     - Riders: Stage 1 and Stage 3 quality checks, cold chain
       maintenance during delivery;
     - All Staff: Compliance with hygiene standards, incident
       reporting.

================================================================================
                    10. HYGIENE AND SANITATION
================================================================================

10.1 PERSONAL HYGIENE.
     - All staff shall maintain high standards of personal cleanliness;
     - Hands shall be washed thoroughly with soap and water:
       * Before handling food;
       * After using the toilet;
       * After handling waste or contaminated materials;
       * After eating, drinking, or smoking;
     - Clean clothing and protective gear (aprons, gloves, hairnets)
       shall be worn during food handling;
     - Jewelry, watches, and loose items shall be removed or secured;
     - Staff with symptoms of illness (fever, diarrhea, vomiting,
       skin infections) shall not handle food and shall report to
       their supervisor.

10.2 FACILITY HYGIENE.
     - All JG Mart premises, warehouses, sorting areas, and storage
       facilities shall be kept clean and sanitary;
     - Regular cleaning and sanitizing schedules shall be established
       and documented;
     - Pest control measures shall be implemented and documented;
     - Waste shall be segregated, contained, and disposed of properly;
     - Toilet and hand-washing facilities shall be available and
       maintained.

10.3 EQUIPMENT HYGIENE.
     - All equipment used for handling, sorting, and packaging shall
       be cleaned and sanitized regularly;
     - Equipment shall be maintained in good working order;
     - Separate equipment shall be used for different product categories
       (e.g., raw meat vs. fresh produce) to prevent cross-contamination.

================================================================================
                    11. PRODUCT RECALL PROCEDURE
================================================================================

11.1 RECALL INITIATION. A product recall may be initiated when:
     - A supplier notifies JG Mart of a safety issue or recall;
     - JG Mart identifies a safety issue through quality checks or
       customer complaints;
     - A regulatory authority orders or recommends a recall;
     - Internal testing or investigation reveals contamination or
       quality failure.

11.2 RECALL ACTIONS. Upon initiating a recall, JG Mart shall:
     - Immediately stop distribution and sale of the affected product;
     - Identify all customers who purchased the affected product;
     - Notify affected customers via WhatsApp, SMS, or phone call;
     - Instruct customers not to consume the affected product;
     - Arrange for collection or safe disposal of the product;
     - Document the recall process and outcomes;
     - Report the recall to relevant authorities if required.

11.3 POST-RECALL REVIEW. After a recall, JG Mart shall:
     - Investigate the root cause of the issue;
     - Review and update supplier approval and monitoring procedures;
     - Implement corrective and preventive actions;
     - Document lessons learned and share with relevant staff and
       partners.

12.2 INTERNAL AUDITS. JG Mart shall conduct internal food safety audits

12.3 CORRECTIVE AND PREVENTIVE ACTION (CAPA). Any non-conformity,

     - Implement immediate corrective action;
     - Develop preventive measures to avoid recurrence;
     - Document the CAPA and monitor effectiveness.

12.4 FEEDBACK LOOP. Feedback from customers, staff, riders, and
     suppliers shall be continuously collected and used to improve
     food safety and quality systems.
