                            RECITALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHEREAS, JG Mart operates a hyperlocal grocery delivery service in the Japan Garden
City, Mohammadpur, Dhaka area, sourcing fresh produce and grocery items from local
vendors including Krishi Market and delivering to customers within approximately
two (2) hours of order placement via WhatsApp-based ordering;

WHEREAS, the Partner owns, operates, or manages a retail shop and/or wholesale
distribution business capable of supplying grocery items, fresh produce, or related
products;

WHEREAS, the Parties desire to establish a partnership whereby the Partner supplies
goods to JG Mart for resale to end customers, subject to the terms and conditions
set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained
herein, the Parties agree as follows:

================================================================================
                        ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS
================================================================================

1.1  "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is
     controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control"
     means the ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting
     securities or equivalent voting interest.

1.2  "Applicable Law" means all statutes, ordinances, regulations, rules,
     orders, decrees, and other laws and requirements of any governmental
     authority applicable to the subject matter of this Agreement, including
     but not limited to the Bangladesh Contract Act, 1872, the Sale of Goods
     Act, 1930, the Companies Act, 1994, the Food Safety Act, and all
     relevant regulations of the Bangladesh Food Safety Authority.

1.3  "Business Day" means any day other than a Friday, Saturday, or public
     holiday in Bangladesh.

1.4  "Commission" means the percentage of the sale price retained by JG Mart
     as described in Article 6.

1.5  "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by
     one Party to the other, whether orally, in writing, or electronically,
     including but not limited to pricing structures, customer data, supplier
     lists, operational processes, and trade secrets.

1.6  "Covered Products" means the category or categories of goods to be supplied
     by the Partner to JG Mart as specified in Schedule A attached hereto.

1.7  "Force Majeure" means any event beyond the reasonable control of a Party,
     including but not limited to acts of God, war, terrorism, riot, fire,
     flood, epidemic, pandemic, strike, lockout, labor dispute, civil commotion,
     government action, or failure of public utilities or transportation.

1.8  "Intellectual Property" means all patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade
     names, logos, service marks, trade secrets, know-how, and all other
     intellectual property rights of any nature throughout the world.

1.9  "Order" means a purchase order or WhatsApp-based order placed by JG Mart
     or on behalf of a customer through the JG Mart ordering system for Covered
     Products.

1.10 "Territory" means the geographic area within which JG Mart operates its
      delivery service, currently encompassing Japan Garden City, Mohammadpur,
      Dhaka, and surrounding localities within a [INSERT RADIUS, e.g., 3 km]
      radius, as may be expanded from time to time.

================================================================================
                    ARTICLE 2: TERM AND TERMINATION
================================================================================

2.1  TERM. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall
     continue for an initial term of three (3) months ("Initial Term"),
     unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Article 2. Upon
     expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically
     renew for successive one (1) month periods ("Renewal Terms"), unless
     either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least fifteen
     (15) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

2.2  TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE. Either Party may terminate this Agreement
     for any reason upon fifteen (15) days' prior written notice to the
     other Party.

2.3  TERMINATION FOR CAUSE. Either Party may terminate this Agreement
     immediately upon written notice if the other Party:

     (a) materially breaches any provision of this Agreement and fails to
         cure such breach within fifteen (15) days after receiving written
         notice specifying the breach;

     (b) becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors,
         or has a receiver, trustee, or liquidator appointed for a substantial
         part of its assets;

     (c) violates any applicable law or regulation in connection with the
         performance of this Agreement;

     (d) engages in fraudulent activity, misrepresentation, or willful
         misconduct in connection with this Agreement (for which termination
         shall be immediate without cure period);

     (e) fails to maintain required licenses, permits, or approvals necessary
         for the performance of its obligations under this Agreement.

2.4  EFFECT OF TERMINATION. Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement:

     (a) All Orders placed prior to the effective date of termination shall
         be fulfilled in accordance with the terms hereof;

     (b) The Partner shall immediately cease using JG Mart's branding,
         trademarks, and Confidential Information;

     (c) Each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential
         Information, except as required by applicable law;

     (d) JG Mart shall settle all outstanding amounts due to the Partner
         within the settlement period specified in Article 7, less any
         applicable deductions for returns, damages, or disputed amounts.

2.5  SURVIVAL. Articles 1, 8, 9, 10, 11, and any other provisions that by
     their nature should survive termination shall survive the expiration or
     termination of this Agreement.

================================================================================
                    ARTICLE 3: SCOPE OF SUPPLY
================================================================================

3.1  PRODUCT CATEGORIES. The Partner agrees to supply the Covered Products as
     listed in Schedule A. The Partner represents that it has the necessary
     expertise, facilities, inventory, and personnel to consistently supply
     Covered Products of the quality and quantity required by JG Mart.

3.2  PRODUCT LIST UPDATES. JG Mart may, from time to time, request that the
     Partner add or remove specific products from Schedule A. Such requests
     shall be made in writing, and the Partner shall use reasonable efforts to
     accommodate such requests within seven (7) Business Days.

3.3  PRICING. The Partner shall supply Covered Products to JG Mart at the
     wholesale rates specified in Schedule B ("Wholesale Rates"). Wholesale
     Rates shall be [fixed for the Initial Term / subject to review every
     thirty (30) days] and shall be communicated to JG Mart at least seven (7)
     days prior to any proposed change. JG Mart reserves the right to
     renegotiate rates if market conditions materially change.

3.4  PRICE TRANSPARENCY. The Partner shall provide JG Mart with a current
     price list at the commencement of this Agreement and shall notify JG Mart
     in writing within twenty-four (24) hours of any change to the price list.

================================================================================
                    ARTICLE 4: COMMISSION STRUCTURE
================================================================================

4.1  COMMISSION RATES. JG Mart shall retain a Commission on the sale of each
     Covered Product. Commission rates shall be determined by product category
     as follows:

     Category A (Grains, Pulses, Cooking Oil, Staples):           5%  - 8%
     Category B (Fresh Fruits & Vegetables):                       10% - 14%
     Category C (Dairy, Eggs, Meat, Fish):                         14% - 18%
     Category D (Packaged Foods, Snacks, Beverages):               8%  - 12%
     Category E (Household Items, Personal Care):                  5%  - 8%

     The exact Commission rate applicable to each product shall be specified
     in Schedule C. JG Mart reserves the right to adjust Commission rates
     within the ranges specified above upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

4.2  CALCULATION. The Commission shall be calculated as a percentage of the
     final retail selling price charged to the customer, inclusive of all
     applicable taxes and delivery fees, or as otherwise agreed in Schedule C.

4.3  SETTLEMENT. The Commission shall be deducted from amounts due to the
     Partner during the weekly settlement process described in Article 7.

4.4  VOLUME BONUSES. JG Mart may, at its sole discretion, offer volume-based
     Commission discounts or bonuses to the Partner. Any such bonuses shall be
     specified in a written addendum to this Agreement.

================================================================================
                    ARTICLE 5: QUALITY STANDARDS
================================================================================

5.1  GENERAL STANDARDS. The Partner shall ensure that all Covered Products
     supplied to JG Mart:

     (a) Are of merchantable quality, fit for human consumption (where
         applicable), and conform to the descriptions and specifications
         provided by the Partner;

     (b) Comply with all applicable food safety, health, and sanitation
         standards under Bangladeshi law, including standards set by the
         Bangladesh Standards and Testing Institution (BSTI) and the
         Bangladesh Food Safety Authority;

     (c) Are free from adulteration, contamination, foreign objects, and
         defects;

     (d) Are properly labeled with batch numbers, expiry dates, manufacturer
         details, and any other information required by Applicable Law;

     (e) Are stored, handled, and transported in a manner that preserves
         their quality and safety.

5.2  CERTIFICATIONS. The Partner shall maintain all necessary business
     licenses, trade licenses, food licenses, and any other certifications
     required by Applicable Law. Upon request by JG Mart, the Partner shall
     provide copies of such licenses and certifications.

5.3  QUALITY INSPECTIONS. JG Mart and/or its agents shall have the right, but
     not the obligation, to inspect the Partner's premises, storage facilities,
     and inventory at any time during normal business hours upon reasonable
     prior notice.

5.4  REJECTED GOODS. If any Covered Products fail to meet the quality
     standards set forth in this Article 5, JG Mart may reject such goods.
     Rejected goods shall be returned to the Partner at the Partner's expense,
     and JG Mart shall not be liable for payment of the Wholesale Rate for
     such goods. Recurring quality failures may constitute grounds for
     termination under Article 2.3.

================================================================================
                    ARTICLE 6: ORDER FULFILLMENT
================================================================================

6.1  ORDER TRANSMISSION. Orders shall be transmitted to the Partner via
     [WhatsApp / JG Mart mobile application / telephone / email] as mutually
     agreed. JG Mart shall use commercially reasonable efforts to provide the
     Partner with accurate and complete order information.

6.2  CONFIRMATION. The Partner shall confirm receipt of each Order within
     fifteen (15) minutes during business hours (8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, seven
     days a week). If the Partner cannot fulfill an Order in whole or in part,
     the Partner shall immediately notify JG Mart, providing the reason and
     any available alternatives.

6.3  PICKUP WINDOWS. The Partner shall make Covered Products available for
     pickup by JG Mart riders during the following windows:

     (a) Morning Slot:  8:00 AM - 10:00 AM
     (b) Afternoon Slot: 12:00 PM - 2:00 PM
     (c) Evening Slot:   4:00 PM - 6:00 PM

     Specific pickup windows shall be assigned per Order based on customer
     delivery slot requirements. The Partner shall ensure that Orders are
     packed, labeled with the customer name and order number, and ready for
     pickup at the commencement of the assigned pickup window.

6.4  PACKAGING. The Partner shall use appropriate packaging materials to
     protect Covered Products during transit, including but not limited to
     ventilated bags for fresh produce, insulated containers for perishable
     items, and secure packaging for fragile goods. JG Mart may provide
     standard packaging materials or require the Partner to procure specific
     packaging at the Partner's expense.

6.5  PREPARATION TIME. The Partner shall complete the preparation and packing
     of each Order within thirty (30) minutes of receiving the Order
     confirmation request.

6.6  UNAVAILABILITY. If a Covered Product becomes unavailable, the Partner
     shall immediately notify JG Mart. JG Mart may substitute a comparable
     product, remove the item from the Order, or cancel the Order in whole
     or in part. In the event of partial fulfillment, the Parties shall
     adjust the Wholesale Rate and Commission accordingly.

================================================================================
                    ARTICLE 7: PAYMENT AND SETTLEMENT
================================================================================

7.1  PAYMENT TERMS. JG Mart shall pay the Partner the Wholesale Rate for all
     Covered Products delivered and accepted by JG Mart customers, less the
     Commission, less any applicable deductions for returns, damages, or
     customer refunds.

7.2  SETTLEMENT SCHEDULE. Settlement shall be processed on a weekly basis
     according to the following schedule:

     (a) Monday through Tuesday: JG Mart shall reconcile the previous week's
         Orders (Wednesday through Tuesday) and calculate amounts due;

     (b) Wednesday: JG Mart shall issue a remittance advice and initiate
         payment via [bank transfer / bKash / Nagad / cash] for the net
         amount due;

     (c) Payment shall be considered complete when funds have been transferred
         to the Partner's designated bank account or mobile financial service
         account.

7.3  INVOICING. The Partner shall submit a weekly invoice to JG Mart via
     [WhatsApp / email / physical delivery] by Tuesday at 5:00 PM for all
     Orders fulfilled during the previous week. The invoice shall include:

     (a) Partner name, address, and trade license / TIN number;

     (b) Invoice number and date;

     (c) Itemized list of all Covered Products supplied, including Order
         number, product name, quantity, unit Wholesale Rate, and total
         Wholesale Rate;

     (d) Total amount due, Commission deducted, and net amount payable;

     (e) Any applicable taxes (VAT / TDS).

7.4  DEDUCTIONS. JG Mart reserves the right to deduct from any payment:

     (a) Amounts attributable to returned, damaged, or defective goods;

     (b) Amounts attributable to customer refunds or chargebacks related to
         Orders fulfilled by the Partner;

     (c) Any penalties or liquidated damages arising under this Agreement;

     (d) Any amounts owed by the Partner to JG Mart under any other agreement
         or arrangement.

7.5  DISPUTED AMOUNTS. If the Partner disputes any deduction or calculation,
     the Partner shall notify JG Mart in writing within seven (7) days of
     receiving the remittance advice. The Parties shall negotiate in good
     faith to resolve the dispute. Unresolved disputes shall be referred to
     dispute resolution under Article 10.

================================================================================
                    ARTICLE 8: EXCLUSIVITY AND PRIORITY
================================================================================

8.1  NON-EXCLUSIVITY. This Agreement does not grant the Partner exclusive
     rights to supply any Covered Products within the Territory. JG Mart
     reserves the right to source Covered Products from other suppliers.

8.2  PRIORITY DURING PICKUP. During the term of this Agreement, the Partner
     shall receive priority consideration for Orders that the Partner has
     confirmed and prepared. Subject to stock availability, JG Mart shall
     endeavor to fulfill Orders from the Partner before sourcing from
     alternative suppliers for the same category of products.

8.3  EXCLUSIVITY DURING PICKUP WINDOWS. During the Partner's assigned pickup
     windows, JG Mart riders shall prioritize pickup from the Partner's
     premises over other non-contracted suppliers, provided the Partner's
     Order is ready for pickup at the scheduled time.

8.4  LOYALTY EXPECTATIONS. While not granting exclusivity, JG Mart expects
     the Partner to prioritize JG Mart Orders and maintain adequate stock
     levels to fulfill anticipated demand. The Partner shall notify JG Mart
     immediately of any anticipated stock shortages that may affect Order
     fulfillment.

================================================================================
                    ARTICLE 9: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
================================================================================

9.1  OWNERSHIP. Each Party retains all right, title, and interest in and to
     its respective Intellectual Property.

9.2  LIMITED LICENSE. The Partner grants JG Mart a limited, non-exclusive,
     royalty-free license to use the Partner's name, logo, and trademarks
     solely for the purpose of identifying the Partner as a supplier of
     Covered Products on the JG Mart platform, marketing materials, and
     customer communications. This license shall terminate upon expiration
     or termination of this Agreement.

9.3  JG Mart BRANDING. JG Mart grants the Partner a limited, non-exclusive,
     royalty-free license to use the "JG Mart" name and logo solely for
     purposes directly related to this Agreement, including labeling Orders
     for JG Mart customers. The Partner shall not use the JG Mart name or
     logo for any other purpose without prior written consent.

9.4  RESTRICTIONS. Neither Party shall register or attempt to register the
     other Party's Intellectual Property in any jurisdiction, nor challenge
     the validity of the other Party's Intellectual Property rights.

================================================================================
                    ARTICLE 10: DISPUTE RESOLUTION
================================================================================

10.1 NEGOTIATION. The Parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute, controversy,
     or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, or the breach
     thereof, through good-faith negotiations between senior representatives
     of each Party. Such negotiations shall commence within seven (7) days
     of a written request by either Party.

10.2 MEDIATION. If the Parties are unable to resolve the dispute through
     negotiation within twenty-one (21) days, the dispute shall be submitted
     to mediation administered by the [Dhaka Chamber of Commerce & Industry
     (DCCI) / Bangladesh Association of Software & Information Services
     (BASIS) / Local Trade Association] under its then-prevailing mediation
     rules. The mediation shall be conducted in Dhaka, Bangladesh, in the
     English and/or Bangla language. The costs of mediation shall be shared
     equally by the Parties.

10.3 ARBITRATION. If mediation fails to resolve the dispute within forty-five
     (45) days, the dispute shall be finally resolved by binding arbitration
     administered by the [Bangladesh International Arbitration Centre (BIAC)
     / Dhaka Chamber of Commerce & Industry (DCCI) Arbitration Committee]
     under its then-prevailing arbitration rules. The arbitration shall be
     conducted in Dhaka, Bangladesh, in the English and/or Bangla language.
     The arbitral tribunal shall consist of one (1) arbitrator mutually
     agreed upon by the Parties, or if no agreement is reached within fifteen
     (15) days, appointed by the administering institution. The award shall
     be final and binding, and judgment upon the award may be entered in any
     court having jurisdiction.

10.4 GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in
     accordance with the laws of the People's Republic of Bangladesh,
     without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

10.5 JURISDICTION. Subject to Article 10.3, the courts of Dhaka, Bangladesh
     shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any matter not subject to
     arbitration.

================================================================================
                    ARTICLE 11: CONFIDENTIALITY
================================================================================

11.1 OBLIGATION. Each Party agrees to keep confidential all Confidential
     Information of the other Party and shall not disclose, publish, or
     otherwise disseminate such Confidential Information to any third party
     without the prior written consent of the disclosing Party, except as
     required by Applicable Law or as necessary for the performance of this
     Agreement.

11.2 EXCEPTIONS. The obligations of confidentiality shall not apply to
     information that:

     (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving
         Party;

     (b) was rightfully in the receiving Party's possession prior to
         disclosure by the disclosing Party;

     (c) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction on
         disclosure;

     (d) is independently developed by the receiving Party without use of
         the disclosing Party's Confidential Information;

     (e) is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order,
         provided the receiving Party gives the disclosing Party prompt
         written notice (where legally permissible) and cooperates in
         seeking a protective order.

11.3 DURATION. The obligations of confidentiality shall survive the
     expiration or termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5)
     years.

13.2 CAP ON LIABILITY. EXCEPT FOR BREACHES OF ARTICLE 11 (CONFIDENTIALITY),

     (b) Any breach of the Partner's representations, warranties, or
         obligations under this Agreement;

     (c) Any negligence, willful misconduct, or violation of Applicable Law
