================================================================================

5.1  DELIVERY PARTNERS. We share your name, phone number, and delivery

     the purpose of fulfilling your order. These partners are required to
     maintain the confidentiality of your information and may only use it

     assist with our operations (e.g., cloud storage, messaging platforms,

     information if required to do so by Applicable Law, regulation, or
     legal process, or if we believe in good faith that such disclosure is
     necessary to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our
     customers or third parties.

5.6  BUSINESS TRANSFER. In the event of a merger, acquisition, sale of
     assets, or other business transfer, your personal information may be
     transferred as part of the transaction. We will notify you of any
     such transfer via WhatsApp or email.

5.7  NO SALE OF PERSONAL DATA. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal
     information to third parties for marketing or any other purposes.
