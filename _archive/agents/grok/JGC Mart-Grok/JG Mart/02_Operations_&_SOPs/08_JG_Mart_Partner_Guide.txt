PART 1: PARTNER PROFILE REQUIREMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

To become a JG Mart partner, you must meet ALL of the following:

□ Located within 2km of Japan Garden City (preferably Krishi Market)
□ WhatsApp Business account active and responsive
□ Able to confirm orders within 15 minutes during business hours
□ Willing to provide products at agreed wholesale + commission pricing
n□ Available for pickup between 10:00-11:00 AM and 5:30-6:30 PM daily
□ Able to maintain consistent quality and stock levels
□ No history of fraud or customer complaints in last 12 months
□ Willing to sign 3-month minimum commitment

PART 3: WEEKLY SETTLEMENT PROCESS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Every Sunday:
1. JG Mart sends weekly order summary
2. Partner verifies all orders and amounts
3. Any discrepancies resolved within 24 hours
4. JG Mart pays via bKash or cash by Tuesday
5. Payment = (Total wholesale value) - (Commission to JG Mart)

Example:
• Weekly orders: 50,000 BDT (your wholesale price)
• Commission (avg 12%): 6,000 BDT
• Your payment: 44,000 BDT

   Party B may serve other customers but agrees to prioritize JG Mart orders

8. PAYMENT
   Weekly settlement every Sunday-Tuesday cycle.
   Payment via bKash or cash.

9. TERMINATION
   Either party may terminate with 15 days notice.
   Immediate termination for fraud, repeated quality issues, or
   failure to fulfill >20% of orders in a week.

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
    Disputes resolved through mutual discussion.
    If unresolved, arbitration by local trade association.

PART 5: PARTNER ONBOARDING CHECKLIST
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BEFORE SIGNING:
□ Visit shop, inspect quality and cleanliness
□ Verify location (within 2km of JGC)
□ Check WhatsApp response time (test message)
□ Review price list vs. market rates
□ Check for existing customer complaints (ask around)
□ Verify business registration (if applicable)

DURING ONBOARDING:
□ Sign Partner Agreement
□ Collect: Shop name, owner name, phone, address, product categories
□ Photograph shop and owner (for internal records)
□ Create Partner ID (P001, P002, etc.)
□ Add to Google Sheet "Partners" tab
□ Add to WhatsApp Business contacts
□ Send welcome message with pickup schedule
□ Schedule first order trial (5-10 items)

AFTER ONBOARDING (Week 1):
□ Daily check-in on order quality
□ Verify pickup times are met
□ Collect customer feedback on partner items
□ Adjust pricing if needed
□ Add partner to "Available Today" catalog
