═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CORE RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Customer trust > speed. Never deliver bad quality.
2. Confirm every item at the partner before leaving the shop.
3. Reject poor quality immediately and inform Manager on WhatsApp.
4. Keep phone charged and reply within 2 minutes during shift.
5. Cash is sacred — count before leaving and after returning.
6. Never argue with partners or customers. Escalate to Manager.
7. Safety first on Ring Road. No rushing in rain or heavy traffic.

• Follow Pickup Checklist strictly

• Report any issue immediately

EVENING SHIFT
• Same process
• Extra care with fish & meat (ice / freshness)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
QUALITY QUICK CHECK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Vegetables / Leafy → Fresh look, no yellowing or soft spots
Fish → Clear eyes, firm body, no strong bad smell
Chicken / Meat → Proper colour, no slime
Rice / Dal / Oil → Sealed, correct brand/weight
Eggs → No cracks

If in doubt → Take photo and ask Manager before accepting.
