═══ FIRST 10 MINUTES (Setup) ═══

□ Open start.html           → Run the Startup Wizard (5 steps, 3 min)
  • Enter your business info
  • Set your prices (AOV 800, commission 11%, delivery 30)
  • Add 1 customer (yourself or a neighbour)
  • Add 1 partner (any Krishi Market shop)
  → Your data is now saved in all the right places.

□ Open ops.html             → Operations Dashboard (1 min)
  • Check that your name and business name appear
  • Look at the break-even tracker
  • Click any app link to verify it opens

□ Open field_ops.html       → Mobile Field Ops (30 sec)
  • Scroll through on your phone
  • Tap "Open WhatsApp" — does it open your chat?
  • Check the partner section

═══ FIRST HOUR (First Real Order) ═══

□ Get your first order via WhatsApp
□ Open 05_Tech_Dashboard/orders.html → Order Tracker (2 min)
  • Click "New Order" → enter customer details
  • Select Morning or Evening slot
  • Set total to what they ordered
  • Status starts at "Received"

□ Process the order:
  • Confirm with partner → click "Confirmed"
  • Pick up from Krishi Market → click "Picked Up"
  • Deliver to customer → click "Delivered"
  • Get payment → click "Completed"

□ After delivery:
  • Open data.html → Data Manager
  • Add customer permanently (if new)
  • Add partner permanently (if new)
  • Click "Export All Data" → save the JSON file

□ Open 05_Tech_Dashboard/dailypnl.html (2 min)
  • Enter: today's date, total orders, total revenue
  • See auto-calculated: costs, profit, margin
  • Check: did you make or lose money today?

═══ FIRST DAY (End of Day) ═══

□ Open 05_Tech_Dashboard/finance.html → Financial Dashboard (2 min)
  • Enter: date, orders, revenue, costs, new customers
  • Check cash position — did it go up or down?

□ Open data.html → Export All Data (30 sec)
  • Click Export → save as jgmart_data_YYYY-MM-DD.json
  → This is your daily backup. Store it safely.

□ Open 12_Templates/REFERRAL_TRACKER.csv (if you have referrals)
  • Add any new referrals from today

□ Review the day:
  • How many orders? _____
  • How much revenue? ৳_____
  • Any issues? ________________________________
  • What will you do better tomorrow? ________________________________

═══ DAILY ROUTINE (Every Day) ═══

Morning (phone):
  field_ops.html → check status, confirm partners

Throughout day:
  orders.html → track each order through lifecycle
  data.html → add new customers and partners

Evening (laptop):
  dailypnl.html → enter numbers, check profit
  finance.html → update cash position
  data.html → Export All Data (backup!)

═══ THE 3 METRICS THAT MATTER ═══

1. ORDERS TODAY     → Target: 3 (Week 1) → 10 (Month 1) → 30 (Month 3)
2. REVENUE TODAY    → ৳414 (3 orders × 138) → ৳1,380 (10 orders) → ৳4,140 (30 orders)
3. CASH POSITION    → Start at 250,000. If it drops below 200K, you're burning too fast.

═══ WHEN THINGS GO WRONG ═══

Phone banned / WhatsApp down:
  → See 11/DAY1_EMERGENCY_KIT.txt
  → Use backup SIM
  → Process orders offline (paper form in the kit)

Can't remember what to do:
  → Open ops.html → click any app
  → Open READY_TO_LAUNCH.txt
  → Open BUSINESS_CONTINUITY_KIT.txt

Data lost / browser cleared:
  → Import your last exported JSON file
  → Open data.html → click Import → select your backup file
  → All customers, partners, and settings restored
