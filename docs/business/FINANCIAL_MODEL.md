## Unit Economics

```
AOV:                    ₹450
Commission (7%):        ₹32
Delivery fee:           ₹40
Total Revenue:          ₹72
Variable cost:          ₹48
Gross Profit:           ₹24
Contribution Margin:   33%
```

**Break-even volume:** ~2,600 orders/month (fixed costs ÷ gross profit per order)
